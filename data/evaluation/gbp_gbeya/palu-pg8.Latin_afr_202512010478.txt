Zɔ̧fa ngɛm tɛ g nɛ tģk tɛ wire.

Go wese r̈ɛ bo ı q̧ kpāa tɔ̧k na;
nɨɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhohgwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owiire
a ozɔ̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

G᷆o wese nɛ bo nʼ yɔng nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛg, nʼ q kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛ.
Zey yege, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ ţk
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ a bhɔ q kpa zɛrɛ.
