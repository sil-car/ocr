Zɔ̧fa ngɛm tɛ ɛ̧ nɛ tgk tɛ wire.

̄̄̄Go wese n̄ɛ̄ b̄ nʼa kp̄a tɔ̧kn̄a,
n̄ɛ ganaa rɛm a kur na.

Ẅɛ d̍hongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozo̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

G᷆o wḛse nɛ̣ bo nʼa̧ vɔng nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ, nʼa̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zev vege, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ vɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ va zangaai a tɛ tģk
kɔ mbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
