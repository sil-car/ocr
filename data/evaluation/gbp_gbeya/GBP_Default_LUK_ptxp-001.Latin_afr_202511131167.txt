Dedhe Nûwen nɛ

LUKE
ɔrɑ̂ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ᷇ Luke

Wire nɛ vɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A vɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nman ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔņ, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ
«Dɛ̂vâmɔ᷇dɛv kɔ᷇ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke vɔrâi,
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go vinaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼạ kidho wen zu dɛ̂vâzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ vɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ wire nɛ tɛvâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ vɔwâ dhonwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼạ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô̂ wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Sɔ̧̧̂dan dɛvâ zan kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛvâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ ɔɔ mbɛti kɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhon owen
ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛvâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nânâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ᷇ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛva zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ̍ wen bhɔ zu fev
kʼạ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼạ kɛngbarâ tɛ ofev.

Tɛ sɔnkov mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̧ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼạ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fevi. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ Kʼɛ
wen usi Dedhe Nû̂wen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
