Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ

LUKE
jɔrĜ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

WVire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wi̍zam zƐ̂rƐ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwvif nâ. A ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ
hq̧ kpasi k̄ɔ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ Zezi-Kristi,
gaŊ sɔnŊ, Owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwvif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti k̄ɔ
«D̂âmɔdɛv kɔ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ omtbɛti riito nɛ Luk᷅e vɔrâi, ̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwvif nâ go inaa ha Tevofili̍.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼạ kidho wen zu dɛ̂âzan kɔ
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âk̀â owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ ɔra nɛ
mtbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ wire nɛ tɛâ wen kidhi owire
nŋɛ vɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam k̄pasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10)).
Nʼạ usÔ Zezi tɛ zan ÔrÔ wire nɛ zɔk ala k̄ɔ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ FƐngâ-
Ş̧̂dan dɛâ zang kɔ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ ɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ŋĝlɛng-ngɛlɛng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nŋɛ dɛâ mɔ tƐ̂ wese nɛ Zezi k̄u̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wven dɔ̂k̄a zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû nu nɔ̂i, nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fev
kʼạ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa̰ kɛngbarâ tɛ ofev.

Tɛ sɔnko mtbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ k̍ʼɛ̧ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa tunɔ ha tɛ
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ k̍ʼɛ̰
wven usi Dedhe Nû̂wen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa
ŋɛ Nzapa baa nƐ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
