Zɔ̧fa ngɛm tɛ nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wire.

™ṣ Go wese ɛ bö 'a̧ kpa tɔ̧k a;
nɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozɔ̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛ bo nʼa yɔng nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧̧, n'a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yege, bo na̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔk
kɔ mbe wirei, nɛ a̧B bhɔ a kpa zɛrɛi.
