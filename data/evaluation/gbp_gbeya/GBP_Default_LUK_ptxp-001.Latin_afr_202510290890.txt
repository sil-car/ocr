LUKE
jɔrɑ̂ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti mɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A vɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, Owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwvif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Dɛ̂vâmɔ̂dɛ kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke vɔrâi, ạ
tɔ̧wâ̂ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ̂ go vinaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼa kidho wen zu dɛ̂âzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ̧ vɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ vɔwâ̂ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam k̄pasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼa usÔ Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala k̄ɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, ok᷇o in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Sɔ̧̂dan dɛâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nâng̰â vɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ̂ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi k̄u̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wven dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼa tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fe
kʼạ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa kɛngbarâ̂ tɛ ofe.

Tɛ sɔnkov mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̧ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼɛ
wven usi Dedhe Nû̂wen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
