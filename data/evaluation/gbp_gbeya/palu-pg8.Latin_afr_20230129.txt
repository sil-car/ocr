Zɔ̧fa ngɛm tɛ ģ nɛ tģk tɛ wire.

̄᷅̄Ɑo wese n̍ɛ̄ b̄ö ņ᷄ kp̄a tɔ̧k̄
nɛ ganaa rɛm a kur na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozo̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛ bo nʼa̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ̧, nʼa̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yege, bo mʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tģk
kɔ mbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
