Dedhe Nṵ̂wen nɑ

LUKE
ꞌrʉ nɑ mbɑti

Gɑk wen zu mbɑti kꞌ̰ Luke

Wire nɑ ꞌra mbɑti nꞌḛ̰ꞌ ꞌ nɑ Luke. A ꞌ nɑ gẍsa wizam zÊrẂẄ, nɑ
ganaa ꞌ nɑ Zuwif nâ. A ꞌra mbɑti nꞌẄꞌ wen usi Dedhe NûẄwen nɑ
hẍ kpasi kǣꞌ̰ nmang-nmang ha owire nɑẄ dhongwaa kꞌ̰ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sꞌn, OWire nɑ gan ꞌ nɑ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ꞌra mbɑti kꞌ̰
«DẂẄâmꞌ̰dɑv kꞌ̰ oBetom» bhꞌ. Nɑ tɑẄ kꞌ̄ ombɑti riito nɑ Luke vꞌrâi, ẍ
tꞌ̄wâ wen ha nma wire nɑ gan ꞌ nɑ ZuWif nâ go vinaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zꞌk Zezi nɑ rip ɑ̄ wantɑ nâ, gʼẍ kidho wen zu dȨẄâzan kꞌ̰
Zezi nẂẄ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɑ zꞌkâ Zezi nɑ ripʼrôi sokʼẍ ꞌra nɑ
mbɑtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɑ zan ôrô Bem kꞌ̰ wire nɑ tɑâ wen kidhi owire
nɑ ꞌwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam kǣpasi kꞌẄ wa (19:10).
Nʼẍ usô Zezi tɑ zan ôrô wire nɑ zꞌk ala kǣꞌ̰ owire nɑ owire zꞌk wa
ôrô gbꞌlꞌ wire gai: Owikꞌlɑ, okô̰o in obem. Luke usô tom nɑ FẂŝẄngâ-
S̄dan dɑâ zang kꞌ̰ omꞌ nɑ Zezi dɑâi bhꞌ. Dedhe Nûwen nɑ Luke
vꞌrâ nɑ mbɑtii duro gan ombɑti sꞌn nɑ wa vꞌra kꞌ̄ Mbe-Ndꞌkɑi.

Luke kẑ̄wꞌ nângâ ꞌꞌ mbɑti kʼɑ̄i nɑ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ngẄlŝẄng-ngƐẄlȨng zu kov kꞌ̰ Zezi, kꞌraa nʼaẍ tꞌ̄wâ wen zu onma mꞌ
nɑ dɑâ mꞌ tẂẄ wese nɑ Zezi kẑwꞌ nângâ tom kʼɑ zûẄ nu nḛ̰ꞌ̰i. A tꞌ̄wâ
wven dꞌ̰ka zu omꞌ nɑ Zezi dɑva zû nu nḛ̰ꞌ̰i, nʼẍ tꞌ̄wâ wen bhꞌ zu fe
kʼẍ zu sangkarâng te in tûnꞌẄwa kʼa kɑngbarâ tɑ ofev.

Tɑ sꞌnko mbɑtii, Luke tꞌ̄wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɑ Zezi dungꞌ
kpa nɑẂẄ nmaa in owiwemꞌ ḱʼɑ wââ dꞌ̰ka-dꞌ̰ka kꞌraa nʼa tunꞌ ha tɑẄ
fei. Luke tꞌ̄wâ wen bhꞌ zu dhongwaa nɑ Zezi dhafa owiwemꞌ Kkʼɑ
wven usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kꞌraa nʼẍ tꞌ̄wâ wen zu dhongwaa
ɗɑ Nzapa baa nẂẄ Zezi tɑ ripzan mɑi.
