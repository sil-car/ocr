Nma wese nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ģ gan sɔn lam na.
Ɔ rɔ̧k nma wire dung ka̧ tɛ wizɛrɛ̍,

.., Koɔ nɔwa yina nɛ wizamı-zɛrɛ ḩq̧ hʼa̧,
nımaꞌa nɛ zɛrɛi gan kɔ ķkufiya̧; ga̧ ɔ̧y, ə̧
nıɛ wey tɛ q̧ gan ga̧ na.
Zey yege, bo ɔ gɔ̧y wende gan sɔ̧danaa hıɔwa ha tɛ a̧,
go tɛ a̧ na̧k oro wire nɛ ɔ nɛ Zɛrɛ fiyo-biri ga̧,
nɛ ɔ rɔ̧k wi si nɛ ̧ dhoɔnɑ̧ lam tɛ tuwa-zamı-zɛrɛ᷅.
