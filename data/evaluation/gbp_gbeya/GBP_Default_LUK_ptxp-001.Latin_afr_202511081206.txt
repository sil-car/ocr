Dedhe Nûwen nɛ

LUKE
yɔrɑ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaꞌa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nman ha owire nɛ̂ dhong̰waꞌa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha yɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Dɛ̂yâmɔ̂᷇dɛy kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke yɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nıma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go yinaꞌa ha Teyofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼạ kidho wen zu dɛ̂yâzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhong̰waꞌa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôꞌi sokʼạ yɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛyâ wen kidhi owire
ŋnɛ yɔwâ dhong̰waꞌa kʼôrô sidhayi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼa̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôꞌo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂n̰â-
Ş̧̂dan dɛyâ zan kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛyâꞌi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
yɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa yɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nâng̰â yɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaꞌa bûrô dhong owen
ŋnɛgɛ̂lɛ̂n̰-ngɛ̂lɛ̂n̰ zu koy kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraꞌa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
ŋnɛ dɛyâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂ɔi. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛya zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fey
kʼạ zu san̰karâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼạ kɛngbarâ tɛ ofey.

Tɛ sɔnkoy mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhong̰waꞌa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kkpa nɛ̂ nmaꞌa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̣ wâꞌâ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraꞌa nʼa tunɔ ha tɛ̂
feyi. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaꞌa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ K'ʼɛ̰
wen usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraꞌa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhong̰waꞌa
ŋnɛ Nzapa baꞌa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
