Nma wese nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧g gan sɔn lamı na.
Ɔ ŗk nma wire dung ka̧ tɛ wizɛrɛi,

.., Kɔɔ nɔwa yina nɛ wizamı-zɛrɛ ha̧q hʼa̧,
nımaa nɛ zɛrɛi gan kɔ kụ̧kufiya; g“a u̧y, x
nıɛ wey tɛ a̧ gan ga̧ na.
Zey yege, bo ɔ ģy wende gan ş̧danaa hıɔwa ha tɛ a,
go tɛ a̧ na̧k oro wire nɛ ɔ nɛ Zɛrɛ fiyo-bri'ga,
nıɛ ɔ ŗ̧k wi si nɛ a̧ dhong lam tɛ tuwa-zam-zɛrɛ̀.
