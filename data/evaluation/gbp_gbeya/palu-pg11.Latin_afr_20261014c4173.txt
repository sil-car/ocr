Ɔ rɔ̧k ɛrɛ zey ŋɛʼrɛ dɛ omɔ nɛ wizam-zɛrɛ
tɔ̧ haʼrɛ nɛ dhcngwara, zey yege,
borɛkɔ-dncŋga a zsrɛ kʼɛrɛ sɔn larŋn.

Wi ngɛm tɛ'ʼi wenkɔ dîoŋngwaa ŋɔwva
yina zɛrɛ gģģ ɔ zang-zang:
kɔ osɔka wire Ɔ Sɛŋɛ,
hɛ kɔ obemm ɔ sɛnɛ buɔ.
Nɛ onuwey zɛrɛ gɛ̧g yoo diɔk.
Zɛy yeq, zɛrɛ gģɛi bo ɔ ngay go wizɛrɛi Ŋy,
nɛ a kin mɛ ha̧ yina hʼa̧ ŋɛ tan mɛ rɨa.
