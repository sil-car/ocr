Zɔ̧fa ngɛm tɛ ȩ̧ nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wire.

Go wese mɛ bo m᷄ a̧ kṵa tɔ̧k na,
nŋɛ ganaa rɛm a kuriị na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a oz̧̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

G᷆o wese nɛ bo nʼ a̧ vɔng nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧g, nʼ a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛ.
Ze ege, bo nʼa̧ si dhonģ, gʼa̧ vɔng nma gere wre,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ a zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wire, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
