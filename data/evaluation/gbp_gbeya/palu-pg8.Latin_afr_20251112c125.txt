Z̰fa ngɛ᷆m tɛ ̰ nɛ tɔ̰k tɛ iḭre.

Go wesɛ r᷆ɛɛ bo᷅ nı᷄᷄a̧̧᷅ ᷅a᷆ t̰̰k ̄a᷆;
nɛ ganaa rɛm a kurɨị̍ ᷆a᷆.

Nɛ dhohgwaa t̰̰k tɛ᷆ Wireɛ
a oz̰̰fa a nŋɛ kuri̍ kʼoro.

Go wese nŋɛ bo nŋʼa ɔnŋg n᷆ma Wiḭrḛ
nɛ ɔ nŋɛ zɛrɛ gɛḛgg, nʼ̰ kpa obedɔk᷄-zɛrɛɨ̍.
Ze ege, bo nʼa̧ si dɨhong, gʼa̧ ɔng nma gere Wịre,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi̍ nɛ ŋ̧a Zaŋgaai̍ a tɛ t̰k
kɔ mbe wịre᷆i, nɛ a ɓhɔ a̰ Kpa Zɛrɛɨ
