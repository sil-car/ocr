Dedhe Nûwen nɛ

LUKE
ɔrɑ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ᷇ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ gạsa wizam zɛ̂rƐ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwvif nâ. A ɔra mbɛti̍ nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ
hạ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan Sɔn, OWire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwvif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti̍ kɔ̂
«Dɛ̂vâmɔ̂dɛv kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke vɔrâi, ạ
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ ZuWif nâ̂ go vinaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ᷇, gʼaạ kidho wen zu dƐ̂vâzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ vɔra nɛ
mtbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛvâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ ɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam k̀pasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10O)).
Nʼaạ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wWire nɛ zɔk ala k᷆ɔƆ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛŝngâ-
Sɔ̧dan dɛvâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛvâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ ɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ŋgƐ̂lɛ̂ng-ngƐ̂lɛng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nŋɛ dɛâ mɔ tƐ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kuụ̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ᷇ɔ᷇i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛŋa zû nu nɔ᷇ɔ᷇i, nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fev
kʼạ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼaạ kɛngbarâ tɛ ofe.

Tɛ sɔnkov mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̰ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼaạ tunɔ ha tɛŝ̂
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼɛ̣
wen usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼaạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa
ŋɛ Nzapa baa nƐ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
