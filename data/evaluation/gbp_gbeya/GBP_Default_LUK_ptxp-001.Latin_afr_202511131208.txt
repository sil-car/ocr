LUKE
ɔrɑ̂ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ᷇ Luke

Wire nɛ vɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A vɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nman-nman ha owire nɛ̂ dhon̰waa kɔ᷇ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔņ, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha vɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Dɛ̂vâmɔ᷇dɛv kɔ᷇ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke vɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go vinaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼa kidho wen zu dɛ̂vâzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhon̰waa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ vɔra nɛ
mıbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ wire nɛ tɛvâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ vɔwâ dhon̰waa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:l0).
Nʼạ̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂n̰â-
Sɔ̧̂dan dɛvâ zan kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛvâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa vɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛ.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ vɔɔ mbɛti kɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhonɛ owen
n̰gɛ̂lɛ̂n̰-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛvâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nânâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ᷇ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛva zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼạ̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fev
kʼạ zu san̰karâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼaạ kɛnbarâ tɛ ofev.

Tɛ sɔnkov mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhonwaa nɛ Zezi dunɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̣ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ᷇ka kɔraa nʼa tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fevi. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhonwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ Kkʼɛ
wen usi Dedhe Nû̂wen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhon̰waa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
