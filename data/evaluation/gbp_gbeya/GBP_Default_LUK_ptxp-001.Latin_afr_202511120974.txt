LUKE
yɑrɔ̄ nŋʉ mbŋti

Gŋk wen zu mbŋti kɑ̄ Luke

Wire nŋ yɑra mbŋti nɑ̄ɑ ɑ nŋ Luke. A ɑ nŋ gaɨsa wizam zŋ̄rŋ̄, nŋ
ganaa ɑ nŋ Zuwif nâ̄. A yɑra mbŋti nɑ̄ɑ wen usi Dedhe Nuûwen nŋ
haɨ kpasi kɑ̄ nmang-nmang ha owire nŋ̄̄ dhongwaa kɑ̄ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɑn, owire nŋ gan ɑ nŋ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha yɑra mbŋti kɑ̄
«Dŋ̄yâmɑ̄dŋy kɑ̄ oBetom» bhɑ. Nŋ tŋ̄ kɑ ombŋti riito nŋ Luke yɑrâ̄i, aʉ
tɑʉwaâ wen ha nma wire nŋ gan ɑ nŋ Zuwif nâ go yinaa ha Teyofiliị.
Luke gan zɑk Zezi nŋ rip ŋʉ wantŋ nâ̄, gʼaɨ kidho wen zu dŋ̄yâzan kɑ̄
Zezi nŋ̄ dhongwaa âkâ owire nŋ zɑkâ̄ Zezi nŋ ripʼrôi sokʼaɨ yɑra nŋ
mbŋtiiị.

Luke uso Zezi tŋ zan ôrô Bem kɑ̄ wire nŋ tŋyâ wen kidhi owire
nŋ yɑwâ̄ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhayi ga wen zam kpasi kɑ̄ wa (19:10).
Naɨ usô Zeziị tŋ zan ôrô wire nŋ zɑk ala kɑ̄ owire nŋ owire zɑk wa
ôrô gbɑlɑ wire gai: Owikɑlŋ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nŋ Fŋ̄ngâ̄-
Sɑ̄dan dŋyâ zang kɑ̄ omɑ nŋ Zezi dŋyâi bhɑ. Dedhe Nuûwen nŋ Luke
yɑrâ nŋ mbŋtiiị duro gan ombŋti sɑn nŋ wa yɑɑra kɑʉ Mbe-Ndɑkŋi.

Luke kuwɑ nângâ yɑɑ mbŋti kʼŋʉi nŋ dhongwaa buûrô dhong owen
ngŋ̄lŋ̄ng-ngŋ̄lŋ̄ng zu koy kɑ̄ Zezi, kɑraa nʼaɨ tɑwâ wen zu onma mɑ
nŋ dŋyâ mɑ tŋ̄ wese nŋ Zezi kuǔwɑ nâ̄ngâ tom kʼŋʉʉ zû nu nɑ̄ɑ̄i. A tɑʉwâ̄
wen dɑka zu omɑ nŋ Zezi dŋya zû nu nɑ̄ɑ̄i, nʼaɨ tɑʉwâ wen bhɑ zu fey
kʼaɨ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɑ̄wa kʼaɨ kŋngbarâ tŋ ofey.

Tŋ sɑnkoy mbŋtiiị, Luke tɑʉwâ wen zu dhongwaa nŋ Zezi dungɑ
kpa nŋ̄ nmaa in owiwemɑ kʼŋʉ wâaâ̄ dɑ̄ka-dɑ̄ka kɑraa nʼaɨ tunɑ ha tʉ̄
feyi. Luke tɑʉwâ wen bhɑ zu dhongwaa nŋ Zezi dhafa owiwemɑ kʼŋʉʉ
wen usi Dedhe Nuûwen ha owireiị. Kɑraa nʼaɨ tɑʉwâ wen zu dhongwaa
nŋ Nzapa baa nŋ̄ Zezi tŋ ripzan mŋi.
