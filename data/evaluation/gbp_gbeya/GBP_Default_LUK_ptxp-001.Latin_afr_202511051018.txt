Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ᷇ Luke

Wire nɛ yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nı̂wen nɛ
hạ̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmanɛ ha owire nɛ̂ dhong̰waa kɔ᷇ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha yɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Dɛ̂yâmɔ᷇dɛy kɔ᷇ oBetomɔ» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke yɔrâi, a
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go yinaa ha Teyofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼạ kidho wen zu dɛ̂yâzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhong̰waa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ yɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ᷇ wire nɛ tɛyâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ yɔwâ dhong̰waa kʼôrô sidhayi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19: 10).
Nʼạ usô̂ Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obemı.. Luke usô̂ tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
S5̧̧dan dɛyâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛyâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nuûwen nɛ Luke
yɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa yɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nân̰â yɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhong̰waa bûrô dhong owen
ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu koy kɔ᷇ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛyâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nânâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ᷇ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛya zû nu nɔ᷇ɔ̂i, nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fey
kʼạ zu sang̰karânɛ te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼạ kɛngbarâ tɛ ofey.

Tɛ sɔnkoy mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhong̰waa nɛ Zezi dunɔ
kpa nɛ̣̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̰ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼạ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
feyi. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼɛ̰
wen usi Dedhe Nı̂wen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhong̰waa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
