Zɔ̧fa ngɛmı tɛ nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wi̍re..

ʼ̃ Go wes rɛ b̄ō ̄ʼa̧ kp̄a tɔ̧k na
ŋɛ gaŋaa rɛm a kurı́ na.

ɛ d̍honɑg̰waa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozɔ̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro..

Gɨ᷆o wese nɛ bo nʼa̧ ɔng nma wi̍re
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ģ, mıʼa̧ kpa obedɔk~-zɛrɛi.
Zev egɛ, bo mʼa̧ si dhonģ, gʼa̧ ɔng nmıa gere wire,
ŋɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ va zangaai̍ a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wirej, nɛ æ̧ bh a̧ kpaa zɛrɛi.
