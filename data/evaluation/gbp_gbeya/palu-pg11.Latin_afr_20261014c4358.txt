Ɔ rɔ̧k ɛrɛ zey nɛʼrɛ dɛ omɔ nɛ wizam-zɛrɛ
tɔ̧ haʼrɛ nɛ dhongwra, zey yege,
boʼrɛkɔ-dncoŋga a zɛrɛ kʼɛrɛ son larn.

Wi ngɛm tɛʼi wenkɔ dîongwaa novwva
yina zɛrɛ gģ̧ ɔ zang-zang:
kɔ osɔka Wire Ɔ Sɛnɛ,
hnɛ kɔ obemm ɔ sɛnɛ b̂nɔ.
Nɛ ocnuwey zɛrɛ gɛ̧g yoo dɔk.
Zy yeqe, zɛrɛ ggɛi bo ɔ ngay go wizɛrɛi Ŋ̧y,
nɛ a kin mɛ ha̧ yina hʼa̧ nɛ tan mɛ rɨa.
