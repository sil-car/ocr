Dedhe ŊNûwen nɛ

LUKE
jvɔrɑ̂ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti mɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ gạsa wizam zê̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ̂. A vɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe NŊû̂wen nɛ
ha kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔŊn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwvif nâ̂i. Luke ha vɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«D̂â̂mɔ̂dɛ kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke vɔrâi, ạ
tɔ̧wâ̂ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwvif nâ̂ go vinaa ha TevOfili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ȩ̧ wantɛ nâ̂, gʼạ kidho wen zu dƐŝ̂â̂zan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa â̂kâ̂ owire nɛ zɔkâ̂ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ vɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛâ̂ wen kidhi owire
nɛ vɔwâ̂ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼạ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ̂-
Sɔ̧dan dɛâ̂ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛâi bhɔ. Dedhe NŊû̂wen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ̂ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke kụwɔ nâ̂ngâ̂ vɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bû̂rô dhong owen
ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ̂ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛâ̂ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kụwɔ nâ̂ngâ̂ tom kʼɛ̧ zû̂ nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i. A tɔ̧wâ̂
wven dɔ᷇ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû̂ nu nƆ̂ɔ̂i, nʼạ tɔ̧wâ̂ wen bhɔ zu fe
kʼạ zu sangkarâ̂ng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa kɛngbarâ̂ tɛ ofe.

Tɛ sɔnko mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ̂ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̧ wâ̂â̂ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼạ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ̂ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼɛ̧
wven usi Dedhe Nû̂wen ha owvirei. Kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ̂ wen zu dhongwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
