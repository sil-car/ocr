Zɔfa ngem tɛ ȩ̧ nɛ tgk tɛ wire.

ꞌ G᷆o wese n̈ɛ̍b̄o̍ꞌ̧᷄ kpa tɔ̧kna;
ŋɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔk tɛ owire
a ozɔ̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛꞌbo nʼa̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧g, nʼa̧ kpa obedɔk-zerɛi.
Zey yege, bo nʼq si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a t᷆ɛ ţk
kɔ m̍be wirei, nɛ æ̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
