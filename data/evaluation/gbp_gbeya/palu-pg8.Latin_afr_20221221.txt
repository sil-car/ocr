Zɔ̧fa ngem tɛ ģ nɛ tgk tɛ wire.

̄̄Ɑ᷆o wese n̄ɛ̈b̄ō̧᷄̄kpa ţkn̄a,
n̄ɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri n̄a.

lNɛ dhongwaa tgk tɛ owire
a oz̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

G᷆o wese nɛbo nʼa̧ yɔn̄g nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zerɛ gɛ̧̧, nʼ̌a̧ kpa obedɔk-zerɛi.
Zey yege, bo nʼ̧ si dh̄ong, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tgk
kɔ mbe wirei, n̄ɛ ʉ̧ bhɔ ̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
