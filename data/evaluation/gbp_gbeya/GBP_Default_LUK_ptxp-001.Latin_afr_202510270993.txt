Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ gsa wizam zƐ̂rƐ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ
ḩ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti k̄ɔ̂
«DƊ̂âmɔ᷇dɛ kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ omtbɛti riito nɛ Luke vɔrâi, ạ
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go vinaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼạ kidho wen zu dƐâzan kɔ̂
Zezzi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âk̀â owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ vɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ ɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam k̄pasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
ŊNʼạ usÔ Zezi tɛ zan Ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala k̄ɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ FƐ̂ngâ-
Sɔ̧̧̂dan dɛâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke kuụ̧wɔ nângâ ɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
nĝlƐng-ngɛ̂lɛng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nŋɛ dɛâ mɔ tƐ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kụ̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wven dɔ̂k̄a zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû nu nɔ̂ɔi, nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fȩ
kʼạ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa̰ kɛngbarâ tɛ ofev.

Tɛ sɔnko mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ k̄ʼɛ̰ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼạ tunɔ ha tɛ
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ k̄ʼɛ̰
wven usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa
ŋɛ Nzapa baa nƐ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
