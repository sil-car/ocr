Nma wese nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧g gan sɔn lam na.
Ɔ rgk nma wire dung ka̧ tɛ wizɛrɛ,

., Koɔ nɔwa yina nɛ wizam-zɛrɛ ha̧a̧ hʼa̧,
nmaa nɛ zɛrɛi gan kɔ Kʉ̧kutiya; gí́a̧ ̧̧y,
nɛ wey tɛ a̧ gan ga̧ na.
Zey yege, bo ɔ gɔ̧y wende gan sɔ̧danaa hɔwa ha tɛ a̧,
go tɛ a̧ na̧k oro wire nɛ ɔ nɛ Zɛrɛ fiyo-biri'ga,
nɛ ɔ rģk wi si nɛ a̧ dhong lam tɛ tuwa-zam-zɛrɛ.
