Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti̍ mɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ gạsa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. AẠ ɔra mbɛti̍ nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe NŊû̂wen nɛ
ha kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, Oowire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwvif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«D̂âmɔ̂dɛ kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke vɔrâi,
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go vinaa ha Tevofili̍.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ ri̍p ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼa kidho wen zu dŝ̂âzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ vɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛvâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ ɔwâ̂ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).

Sɔ̧dan dɛâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi̍ dɛâi bhɔ. Dedhe NŊûwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ vɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kuwɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wven dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fe
kʼạ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa kɛngbarâ tɛ ofe.

Tɛ sɔnko mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̧ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼɛ
wven usi Dedhe Nû̂wen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi̍.
