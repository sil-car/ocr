Ɔɔ ro̧k ɛrɛ zy ŋıɛ̍́rɛ dɛ ōmɔ nɛ wi̍zam-zɛrɛ
t̂ɔ̧ haʼrɛ nɛ dhœngwara̧, zey yege,
bo“rɛ ɔ-dncŋıga ha zɛrɛ ḱ ɛrɛ sɔn !̍afn~

Wi ngɛmm tɛʼî wenkɔ d́orngwa novva
yi̍na zɛfɛ ģ̧̧̧ Ɔ zang-z̍ang:
kɔ osɔk̍a̰ Wire Ɔ Ɛɛnɛ,
hɛ kɔ obemı ɔ ssns ɓ̍ıɔ.
N̂ɛ onuwey zɛrɛ gɛ̧g yoo d̍ɔk.
Z ḛy yege, zɛrɛ gɛ̧gi bo ɔ ngay go wizɛrɛi ̧y,
nɛ a kiịn mɛɛ ha̧ yina hʼa̧ nɛ tan mɛ rɨa.
Mɛ nɛ tɛ wizam-zɛrɛ, nɛ ńa̧ tɔ̧ mɔra h́a mɛ!
