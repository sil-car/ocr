Zɔ̧fa ngɛm tɛ ȩ̧ nɛ tɔ̧k̍ tɛ wire.

'Go wesɛ nɛ bo ʼa̧ kṵa tɔ̧k na,
ŋıɛ ganaa rɛm a kurị̂ na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a oz̧̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

G᷆o wese nɛ bo nʼ̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧g, nʼ a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yɛgɛ, bo nʼ́a̧ si dhon, gʼa̧ yɔng nmıa gere Wire,
nɛɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wire, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
