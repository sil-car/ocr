Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A̰ ɔ nɛ gsa wizam zê̂rƐ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ̂. A̰ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
h kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«D̂âmɔ̂dɛ kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke ɔrâi, ạ̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ g̰an ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go vinaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼa kidho wen zu dƐ̂âzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ ɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ ɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhai ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
ŊNʼạ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala k̄ɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Ş̧dan dɛâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke kụ̧wɔ nâng̰â ɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ŋnĝlɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu nna mɔ
ŋnɛ dɛâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kụwɔ nâng̰â tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wven dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼạ tɔwâ wen bhɔ zu fev̧
kʼạ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa kɛngbarâ tɛ ofev.

Tɛ sɔnko mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̣wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̰ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fei. Luke tɔwâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼɛ̰
wven usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̰wâ wen zu dhongwaa
ŋɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
