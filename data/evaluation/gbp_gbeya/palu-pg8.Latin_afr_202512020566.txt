Zɔ̧fa ngɛm tɛ g nɛ tģk tɛ wire.

Go wese mɛ bo m a̧ kpa᷄ tɔ̧k na;
nıs ganaa rɛm a kurị na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a oz̧̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛ bo nʼa̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛg, nʼ a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yɛgɛ, bo mʼa̧ si dhong, g'ʼa̧ yɔng nma gere Wire,
nıɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ t;̧k
kɔ mıbe wirej, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
