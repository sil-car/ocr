Dedhe Nûwen nꞋ

LUKE
yɛrẅ nꞋ mbꞋti

GꞋk wen zu mbꞋti kɛɓ Luke

Wire nꞋ yɛra mbꞋti nɛɓɛ ɛ nꞋ Luke. A ɛ nꞋ gƊɗsa wizam zêɓrꞋɓ, nꞋ
ganaa ɛ nꞋ Zuwif nâ. A yɛra mbꞋti nɛɓɛ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nꞋ
haɗ kpasi kɛɓ nman-nmanɗ ha owire nꞋɓ dhongwaa kɛɓ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɛn, owire nꞋ gan ɛ nꞋ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha yɛra mbꞋti kɛɓ
«̃ƁꞋɓyâmɛdꞋy kɛɓ oBetom» bhɛ. NꞋ tꞋɓ kɛɗ ombꞋti riito nꞋ Luke yɛrâi,
tɛɗwâ wen ha nma wire nꞋ gan ɛ nꞋ Zuwif nâ go yinaa ha Teyofili.
Luke gan zɛk Zezi nꞋ rip g wantꞋ nâ, gʼa kidho wen zu dꞋɓyâzan kɛɓ
Zezi nꞋɓ dhongwaa âkâ owire nꞋ zɛkâ Zezi nꞋ ripʼrôi sokʼƊ yɛra nꞋ
mbꞋtii.

Luke uso Zezi tꞋ zan ôrô Bem kɛɓ wire nꞋ tꞋyâ wen kidhi owire
nꞋ yɛwâ dhonĝwaa kʼôroô sidhayi ga wen zam kpasi kɛɓ wa (19:10).
Nʼa usô Zezi tꞋ zan ôrô wire nꞋ zɛk ala kɛɓ owire nꞋ owire zɛk wa
ôrô gbɛlɛ wire gai: OwikɛlꞋ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nꞋ FꞋɓngâ-
Sɛɗdan dꞋyâ zangɗ kɛɓ omɛ nꞋ Zezi dꞋyâi bhɛ. Dedhe Nûwen nꞋ Luke
yɛrâ nꞋ mbbꞋtii duro gan ombꞋti sɛn nꞋ wa yɛra kɛɗ Mbe-NdɛkꞋi.

Luke kⱭɗwɛ nângâ yɛɛ mbbꞋti kʼꞋɗi nꞋ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ngꞋɓlꞋɓng-ngꞋɓlꞋɓng zu koy kɛɓ Zezi, kɛraa nʼaɗ tɛɗwâ wen zu onma mɛ
nꞋ dꞋyâ mɛ tꞋɓ wese nꞋ Zezi kⱭɗwɛ nângâ tom kʼꞋɗ zû nu nɛɓɓi. A tɛɗwâ
wen dɛǣka zu omɛ nꞋ Zezi dꞋya zû nu nɛɓɛɓi, nʼaɗ tɛɗwâ wen bhɛ zu fey
kʼƊ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɛɓwa kʼa kꞋngbarâ tꞋ ofey.

TꞋ sɛnkoy mbbꞋtii, Luke tɛɗwâ wen zu dhongwaa nꞋ Zezi dungɛ
kpa nꞋɓ nmaa in owiwemɛ kʼꞋɗ wââ dɛka-dɛǣka kɛraa nʼaɗ tunɛ ha tꞋɓ
feyi. Luke tɛɗwâ wen bhɛ zu dhongwaa nꞋ Zezi dhafa owiwemɛ KʼʼꞋ̂
wen usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɛraa nʼƊɗ tɛɗwâ wen zu dhongwaa
nꞋ Nzapa baa nꞋɓ Zezi tꞋ ripzan mꞋi.
