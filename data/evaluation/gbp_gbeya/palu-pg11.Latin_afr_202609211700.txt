Ɔ ro̧k ɛrɛ zəy nɛ'rɛ dɛ omɔ nɛ wizam-zɛrɛ
tɔ̧ haʼrɛ nɛ dhonıgwara, zey yege,
bo'rɛ o-dnoŋnga Aa zɛrɛ kíɛrɛ sɔn !arn:-

MWi ngɛm tɛʼi wenkɔ dtonıgwaa nɔʉa
yina zɛfɛ gɛg Ɔ zang-zang:
kɔ osɔka wire ɔ :ɛnɛ,
hɛ kɔ obemrı ɔ sɛnɛ ɓhıɔ.
Nɛ onuwey zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛg yoo dɔk.
Zɛy yege, zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛgi bo ɔ ngay go wizɛrɛi ̧y,
nɛ a kin mɛ ha̧ yina h'a̧ nɛ tan mɛ ra.
Ms nɛ tɛ wizam-zɛrɛ, nɛ n'a̧ tɔ mbɔra a mɛ!
