ɔrɑ̂ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A vɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nuûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmaņ ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Dɛ̂vâmɔ̂dɛv kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke vɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go vinaa ha Teofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ȩ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼa kidho wen zu dɛ̂vâzan kɔ
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ riprôi sokʼa̧ vɔra nɛ
mɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛvâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ vɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhai ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:1O).
Nʼa usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Ş̂dan dɛvâ zaņ kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛvâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nuûwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ ɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa buûrô dhoņ owen
nĝ̰lɛ̂nģ-nĝ̰lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛvâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kụ̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ᷇̂âi. A tɔ̧wâ
wven dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛva zû nu nɔ̂âi, nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fev
kʼa zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa kɛngbarâ tɛ ofe.

Tɛ sɔnkov mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nḛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̧ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa̧ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ Kkʼɛ
wen usi Dedhe Nuûwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
