2 rggk ɛrɛ zey nɛʼrɛ dɛ omɔ nɛ wizam-zɛrɛ
tɔ̧ haʼrɛ nɛ dhongvara, zey yege,
borɛ-kɔ-nonga a zɛrɛ k'ɛrɛ sɔì ɨrn.

Wi ngɛm tɛʼi wenkɔ dongwaa nɔʉʉa
yinʉ Zɛrɛ g̈́ ɔ zang-zang:
kɔ oOɔka Wire ɔ ɛɛnɛ,
nɛ kɔ oberm ɔ sɛnɛ ɨìɔ.
Nɛ onuwey zɛrɛ ģ yoo dɔk.
Zey yege, zɛrɛ gɛi bo ɔ ngay go wizɛńrɛi ɔĉ,
nɛ a kin mɛ ha̧ yina hʼ'a̧ nɛ tan mɛ rìa.
Mɛ nɛ tɛ ʉizam-zɛrɛ, nɛ nʼa̧ tɔ̧ mbɔra ha mɛ!
