Zɑʉfa ngŋm tŋ ʉ nŋ tɑk tŋ wire.

Go wesə ᷇ŋꞌ o ǽʼaɨɨɨ kpa tɑɨk ı̰a̰;
nŋ ganaa rŋm a kuriị na.

Nŋ dhongwvaa tɨk tŋ owire
a ozıfa a nŋ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nŋʉ bo nʼaɨ yɑng nma wire
nŋ ɑ nŋ zŋrŋ gʉg, nʼaɨ kpa oǒedɑk-zŋrŋi.
Zey yege, bo nʼaɨ si dhong, gʼaɨ yɑng nmıa gere wi᷇re,
nŋ obedɑk-zŋrŋi nŋ ya zangaai a tŋ tɑk
kɑ mbe wire]i, nŋ aʉ bhɑo aɨ kpa zŋrŋi.
