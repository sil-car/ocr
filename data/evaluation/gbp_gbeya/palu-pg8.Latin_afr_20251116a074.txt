Zɔ̧fa ngɛmı tɛ ɛ nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wire.

G᷆o wese rɛ bo w a̧ k᷅a tɔ̧k na
ŋɛɛ ganaa rɛmm a kurị na.

Nɛ dhonwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozɔ̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Gɨ᷆o wese nɛ bo nʼaa̧ vɔng nma wi̍re
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧g, n' a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛ.
Ze gegɛ, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ vɔng nma gere wire
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ va zangaꞌai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛꞌi.
