Dedhe Nuwen nɛ

LUKE
ɔɔrɑ nɛ mbɛti

Gɨɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ Luke

Wire nɛ vɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaꞌa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A vɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmanɛ ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaꞌa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha vɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Dɛ̂vâmɔ᷇dɛ kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke vɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go vinaa ha Teofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ̧, gʼa̧ kidho wen zu dɛ̂vâzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaꞌa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼa̧ vɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛvâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ vɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhai ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10)).
Nʼa̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ̧, okôo in obem.. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Ş̧̂dan dɛvâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛvâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
ɔɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa vɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛꞌi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ vɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhonɛg owen
nɛgɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu ko kɔ̂ Zezi̧, kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛvâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛva zû nu nɔ̂i, nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fev
kʼa zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa kɛngbarâ tɛ ofe.

Tɛ sɔnko mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kɛpa nɛ̂ nmaꞌa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̣ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa̧ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaꞌa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼɛ
wven usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa
ŋnɛ Nzapa baꞌa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
