Zɔ̧fa ngɛmı tɛ ȩ; nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wire.

Go wese r̄ɛ boi᷄́a̧ kpā tɔ̧k na,
nıɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhohgwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a oz̧̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Gɨ᷆o wese nɛ bo nʼa̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̣g, n' a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yege, bo na̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nmna gere Wire,
nɛɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛti.
