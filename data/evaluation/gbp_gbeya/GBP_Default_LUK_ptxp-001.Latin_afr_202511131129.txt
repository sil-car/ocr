Dedɨhe Nuûwen nɛ

LUKE
ɔɔrɑ̂ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ Luke

Wire nɛ vɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A vɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nman ha owire nɛ̂ dhong̰waa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha vɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Dɛ̂vâmɔdɛv kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke vɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go vinaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼạ kidho wen zu dɛ̂vâzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ vɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛvâ wen kidhhi owire
nɛ vɔwâ dhong̰waa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼạ̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ng̰â-
Sɔ̧̂dan dɛvâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛvâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa vɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ vɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
nĝlɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛvâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nânmgâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛva zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fev
kʼạ zu sang̰karâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼạ kɛngbarâ tɛ ofev.

Tɛ sɔnkov mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhong̰waa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa̧ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fevi. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼɛ
wen usi Dedɨhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhong̰waa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
