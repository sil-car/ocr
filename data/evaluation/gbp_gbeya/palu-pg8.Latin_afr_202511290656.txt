Zɔ̧fa ngɛm tɛ g nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wire.

”Go wese rɛ bo᷇ m᷄ a̧ kp̄a tɔ̧k na;
ŋɨɛ ganaa rɛm a kurị na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a oz̧̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛ bo nʼ a̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧g, n' a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yɛgɛ, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere Wire,
nɛɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
