Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ᷇ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ gsa wizam zƐ̂rƐ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ
hạ kpasi kɔ̂ nmnang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ᷇ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«DƐ̂âmɔ᷇dɛ kɔ᷇ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke ɔrâi, ạ̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go inaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼa̰ kidho wen zu dƐ̂âzan kɔ᷇
Zezi nƐ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼa̧ ɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ᷇ wire nɛ tɛâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ ɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼạ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okô̂o in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ FƐ̂ngâ-
Sɔ̂dan dɛâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra ķ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke kụ̧wɔ nâ̂ngâ̂ ɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛi nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ŋnĝlƐ̂ng-ngɛ̂l̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼa tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛâ̂ mɔ tƐ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kụ̧wɔ nâ̂ngâ tom kʼģ zû̂ nu nɔ̂̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ᷇ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛva zû nu nɔ̂̂i, nʼa tɔwâ wen bhɔ zu fev̧
kʼa zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa̰ kɛngbarâ tɛ ofev.

Tɛ sɔnko mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̰wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmnaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ᷇ka kɔraa nʼa tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼɛ̰
wen usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̰wâ wen zu dhongwaa
ŋnɛ Nzapa baa nƐ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
