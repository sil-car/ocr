Ɔ rg̰k nma wire dung ka̧ t̍ɛ wizɛrɛi,

.. Kɔɔ nɔwa yi̍na nɛ wı̍zam-zɛrɛ haa̧
nmaa nɛ zɛrɛi gan kɔ ķ̈kutiya;ʼgʼa̧ ɑ̧y, ̃
nɛ wey tɛ a̧ gan ga̧ na.
Zey yege, bo ɔ gɔ̧y wend̍e gan sɔ̧ɑanaa hɔwa ha tɛ a,
go tɛ a̧ na̧k oro wire nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ fiyo-briꞌga,
nɛ ɔ rģk wi si nɛ a̧ dhong lam tɛ tuwa-za᷇m-zɛ̍rɛ᷆.
