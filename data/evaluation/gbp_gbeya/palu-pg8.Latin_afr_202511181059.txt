Zɔ̧fa ngɛmı tɛ ȩ̧; nɛ tɔɑ̰k tɛ wire.

~ Go wesæ mɛ boiʼ́ a̧ kpāa tɔ̧k na,
nɛ ganaa rɛm a kurị na.

Nɛ dhonɑg̰waa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozɔ̧̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

G᷆o wese nɛ bo nʼa̧ ɔng nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ Zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ̧, nʼ a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zȩ ɛgɛ, bo nʼ a̧ si dong, gʼa̧ ɔng nmıa gere Wire,
nɛɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ a zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ a̧̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
