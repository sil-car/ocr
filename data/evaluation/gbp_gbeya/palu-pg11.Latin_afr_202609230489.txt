ƆƆ ro̧k ɛrɛ zey nıɛ'rɛ dɛ omɔ nɛ wizam-zɛrɛ
ţ haʼrɛ nɛ dhcngwara, zey yege,
bo“rɛ kɔ-dcŋıga hıa zɛrɛ k'ɛrɛ sɔn !amn-

Wi nıgɛm tɛʼi wenɛkɔ diongwaa nɔwvaæ
yimıa zɛrɛ gȩ̧̧g Ɔ zang-zang:
kɔ osɔka Wire ɔ Ɛɛnɛ,
hɛ kɔ obemmı ɔ sɛɛnɛ bɨ̍ıɔ.
ɛ cnuwey zɛrɛ gɛ̧̣̣g yoo di̍ɔk.
Zey yegɛ, zɛrɛ ģ̧gi bo ɔ ngay go wizɛrɛi Q̧y,
nɛ a kin mɛɛ ha̧ yina h'a̧ nɛ tan mɛɛ rɨa.
Mɛ nɛ tɛ wizam-zɛrɛ, nıɛ n'a̧ tɔ̧ mɔra ha mɛ!
