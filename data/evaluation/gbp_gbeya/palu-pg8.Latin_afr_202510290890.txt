Zɔ̧fa ngɛmı tɛ ̧ nɛ tɔ̧k̍ tɛ wvire.

ʼ ʼGo wes n̈ɛ b̃o m̃ʼa̧ kpa tɔ̧k ma;
mɨɛ g̰anaa rɛm a kurıị́ na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k̍ tɛ owire
a ozɔ̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

G᷆o wese nɛ̰ bo nʼa̧ vɔng nma wvire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ̧, mʼạ̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Ze egɛ, bo mʼa̧ si dhonɑ̧, gʼa̧ vɔng nmıa gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ va zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ ̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
