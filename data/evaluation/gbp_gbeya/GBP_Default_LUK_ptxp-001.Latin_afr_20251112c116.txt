LUKE
Xɔrɑ̂ nɛ mɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̰sa wizam zɛ̂rɛl̂, nŋɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nɑû̂wen nŋɛ
ha̰ kpasi kɔ̂ nımang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi̍-K᷄risti̧
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Dɛ̂âmɔ̂dɛ kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̰ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke ̧ɔɔrâÂâi, a̰
tɔ̰wâ wen ha ıma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go inaa ha Tȩofili̍.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̰ wantɛ nâ, gʼa̧ kidho wen zu dɛ̂âzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼa̧ ɔra nŋɛ
mbɛti̍.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ ɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhai ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼa̧ usô Zezi tɛ zanŋ Ôôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nŋɛ owire zɔk wa̰
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂nŋ̧gâ-
Sɔ̂dan dɛŋâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛŋâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nɑ̂wen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii̍ duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔɔra kɔ̰ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ ɔɔ mbɛti kʼi nɛ dhongwaa bûÊrô dhong owenŋ
ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu ko̧ kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̰wâ wen zu onŋma mɔ
nɛ dɛŋâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kuṵ̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i. A tɔ̰̰wâ̂
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼa̧ tɔ̰wâ wen bhɔ zu fȩ
kʼa̧ zu sangkarâng te in tû̂nɔ̂wa kʼa̰ kɛngbarâ tɛ Ofȩ̧.

Tɛ sɔnko mbɛtii̍, Luke tɔ̰wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dunŋ̧gɔ
kpa nɛ̂ ımaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̰ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa̧ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fei. Luke tɔ̰wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼ̰̰
wen usi Dedhe Nɑ̂̂wen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̰wâ wen zu dhonŋgwaɑ
nŋɛ Nzapa baa nŋɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzanŋ mɛi.
