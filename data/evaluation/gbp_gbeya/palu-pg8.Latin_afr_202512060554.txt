Z,fa ngɛm tɛ ȩ̧ nɛ tģk tɛ wire.

Go wese m̍ɛ bo᷇ w᷄ a̧ kpa ţ̧k na,
nıɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhongwaa ţ̧k tɛ owire
a ozu̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛ bo nʼa̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛg, n'a kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yege, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa᷇ yɔng nmıa gere Wwire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a᷇ tɛ !̧̧k
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
