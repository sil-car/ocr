Z ̧fa ngɛm tɛ ȩ nɛ tģk tɛ wire.

Go wese mɛ b̄o ımʼa̧ kpa᷄ ţ̧k ma,
nıɛ ganaa rɛm a kurî na.

ɛ dhongwaa ţ̧k tɛ owire
a oz̧̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Gɨ᷆o wese nɛ bo nʼa̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gȩg, nʼ a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yege, bo nʼa̧ si dhonģ, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere Wre,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ ţk
kɔ mıbe wrei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
