Zɔ̧fa ngem tɛ ȩ̧ nɛ tgk tɛ wire.

̄Ɑo wese n̍ɛ̍ɓ̃ōn᷄a̧᷄kp ţkma,
n̍ɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tgk tɛ owire
a oz̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Ḡo wese nɛ̣bo nʼa̧ vɔn̍g nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ̧, n̍a̧kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zev vege, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ vɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ va zangaai a t̍ɛ tgk
kɔ mbe wirei, nɛ æ̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
