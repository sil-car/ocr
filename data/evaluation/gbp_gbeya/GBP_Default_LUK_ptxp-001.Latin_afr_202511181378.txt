Dedhe Nûwen nɛ

LUKE:
ɔrɑ̂ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A̰ ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zḛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A̰ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmaņ ha owire nɛ̂ dhonģwaa kɔ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Df̂âmɔ̂dɛ kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ omıbɛti riito nɛ Luke ɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go inaa ha Teofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼa̧ kidho wen zu dɛ̂vâzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhonģwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ riprôi sokʼa̧ ɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ ɔwâ dhoņwaa kʼôrô sidhai ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼa̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Sɔ̧̂dan dɛâ zanģ kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mıbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mıbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ ɔɔ mbɛti kgi nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhonģ owen
nıgḛ̂lɛ̂nģ-ngḛ̂lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ķwɔ nângâ tom kʼģ zû nu nɔ̂ɔ᷇i. A̰ tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ᷇ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fe
kʼạ̧ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa̧ kɛngbarâ tɛ ofe.

Tɛ sɔnko mbɛtii,̧ Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhong̰waa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nḛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼg wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa na̧ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼg
wven usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhong̰waa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
