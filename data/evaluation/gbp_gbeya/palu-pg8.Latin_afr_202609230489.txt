Zɔ̧fa ngɛmı tɛ ģ nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wire.

Go wese mɛ bo m' ģ kṵa tɔ̧k ma;
nıɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhohgwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a oz̧̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛ bo n'a̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̣g, n' a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yegɛ, bo n'̧ si dhong, g'a̧ yɔng nma gere Wire,
nɛɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
