Zɔ̧fa ngɛmı tɛ ȩ nɛ tɑ̧k̍ tɛ wire.

Go wese r̈ɛ b̄o ᷄ ̧ kpa᷄ tɔ̧k ma;
ɨɛ ganaa rɛm a kurị na.

Nɛ dhohģwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozu̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛ bo nʼ̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gȩg, Nʼ ̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yegeɛ, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere Wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
