Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ

LUKE
ɔvɔrɑ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A ɔra mbbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbbɛti kɔ
«Dɛ̂âmɔ̂dɛv kɔ̂ oBetom»› bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke ɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go vinaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼa̧ kidho wen zu dɛ̂âzan kɔ
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼa̧ ɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezii tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛvâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ vɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10)).
Nʼa̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem.. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Sɔ̧̧̂dan dɛvâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛvâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ ɔɔ mbbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛvâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhhɔ zu fe
kʼa̧ zu sangkarân te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa̧ kɛngbarâ tɛ ofev.

Tɛ sɔnkoɔ mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̧ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa̧ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼɛ̰
wen usi Dedhe Nû̂wen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa
ŋɛ Nza̧pa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
