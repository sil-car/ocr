Dedhe Nɗ̂wen nɛ

LUKE
vɔrɑ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ᷇ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ gạsa wizam zƐ̂rƐ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ̂. A ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe N̂wen nɛ
hạ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nƐ dhongwaa kɔ᷇ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâ̂i. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«ƊƐâmɔ᷇dɛ kɔ᷇ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tƐ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke ɔrâ̂i, a
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ̂ go vinaa ha Teofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ģ wantɛ nâ̂, gʼa kidho wen zu dƐ̂âzan kɔ᷇
Zezi n̂ dhongwaa â̂kâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼa ɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ᷇ wire nɛ tɛâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ ɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôro sidhai ga wen zam kpasi kɔ᷇ wa (1 9:10).
Nʼạ uso Zezi tɛ zan ôro wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
oroô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okƏo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ FƐ̂ngâ̂-
Sɔdan dɛâ zang kɔ᷇ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛâi bhɔ. Dedhe N̂wen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ̂ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra ķ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke kụwɔ nâ̂ngâ̂ ɔɔ mbɛti kʼi nɛ dhongwaa bɑrô dhong owen
nĝlƐ̂ng-nĝlƐ̂ng zu kov kɔ᷇ Zezi, kɔraa nʼa tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛâ̂ mɔ tƐ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kqwɔ nâ̂ngâ tom kʼģ zû̂ nu nƆ᷇̂̂i. A tɔ̧wâ̂
wen dɔ᷇ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû nu nƆ̂̂i, nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fev
kʼa zu sangkarâ̂ng te in tû̂nɔ̂wa kʼa kɛngbarâ̂ tɛ ofev.

Tɛ sɔnko mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧waâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nƐ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼȩ̧ wâ̂â dɔ᷇̂ka-dɔ᷇ka kɔraa nʼa tunɔ ha tƐ
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼg
wen usi Dedhe Nwen ha owireɨi. Kɔraa nʼa tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nƐ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
