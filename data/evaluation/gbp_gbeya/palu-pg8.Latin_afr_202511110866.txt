Zɛɗfa ngꞋmı tꞋ gɗ nꞋ tɛɗkụ tꞋ wire..

Go wese Ꞌ ǒoɑ ı̧ aɗ kƆɑā tɛɗk maɗ;
̄ɑꞋ ganaa rꞋm a kurı naɗ.

NꞋ dhonĝwaa tɛɗk tꞋ owire
a ozɛɗfa a nꞋ kuri kʼoro..

G᷇o wese nꞋ bo nʼ aɗ yɛnĝ nma wire
nꞋ ẽ nꞋ zꞋrꞋ gꞋɗꞋ, ʼ aɗ kpa obedɛk~zꞋrꞋi.
Zey yege, bo nʼ aɗ si dhonẅ̂ɗ, gʼaɗ yɛng nma gere wire,
nꞋ obedɛk-zꞋrꞋi nꞋ ya zangaa a tꞋ tɛɗk
kɛ mıbe wirei, nꞋ æɗ bhɛ aɗ kpa zꞋrꞋi.
