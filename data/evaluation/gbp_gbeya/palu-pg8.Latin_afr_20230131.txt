Zɔ̧fa ngɛm tɛ ɛ̧ nɛ tg̰k tɛ wire.

̄̄᷆Go wese n̈ɛ̄ ɓ̄ō nʼa kp̄a tɔ̧kn̄a,
nɛ ganaa rɛm a kur na.

Nɛ d̍hongwaa tɔ̰k tɛ owire
a ozo̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛꞌbo nʼa̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ̧, nʼa̧ kpa o̍bedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yege, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ o̍bedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔ̰k
kɔ mbe wrei, nɛ a̧ b̍hɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛɨ.
