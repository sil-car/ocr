Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaꞌa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nman ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaꞌa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kɑristi,
gan sɔn, oOwire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha yɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Dɛ̂yâmɔ̂dɛy kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke yɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nıma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go yinaꞌa ha Teyofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼa̧ kidho wen zu dɛ̂yâzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̣̂ dhongwaꞌa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ rip”ʼrôi sokʼa̧ yɔra nɛ
mıbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛyâ wen kidhi owire
ŋɛ yɔwâ dhonwaꞌa kʼôrô sidhayi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼa̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obemı. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂̂ngâ-
Ş̧̂dan dɛyâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛyâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
yɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa yɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nânâ yɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaꞌa bûrô dhong owen
ŋnɛgɛ̰̰̂lɛ̂n-nɛgɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu koy kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraꞌa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
ŋɛ dɛyâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂ɔi. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛya zû nu nɔ̂ɔi, nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fey
kʼa̧ zu sanɛkkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa kɛngbarâ tɛ ofey.

Tɛ sɔnkoy mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaꞌa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kɔpa nɛ̣̣̂ nmaꞌa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̣ wâꞌâ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraꞌa nʼa̧ tunɔ ha tɛ̣
feyi. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaꞌa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ k'ʼɛ
wen usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraꞌa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaꞌa
ŋɛ Nzapa baꞌa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
