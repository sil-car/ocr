Ɔ rɔ̰k na wire dung ka̧ tɛ wizɛḛ̰rɛ.,

,, Kɔ nɔwa yina nɛ wizamı-zɛrɛ ha̧q̧ hʼa̧,
ŋımaa nɛ zɛrɛi ɑg̰an kɔ ķkuiya; gʼa̧ ̧y, ̌
nıɛ wey tɛ q̧ gan ga̧ na.
Zey yege, bo ɔ gɔ̧y wend᷆e gan şd᷆a̧naa hɔwa ha tɛ a,
go tɛ a̧ na̧k oro wire nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ fiyo-bɨ᷇riga̧,
ŋɛ ɔ rɔ̧k wi᷆ si nɛ a̧ dhonɑ̧ lam tɛ tuwa-zamı-zɛrɛ̀.
