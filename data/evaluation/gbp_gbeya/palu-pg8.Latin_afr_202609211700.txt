Zɔ̧fa ngem tɛ g nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wire.

o wese rɛ Bo a̧ kga tɔ̧k na;
nɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozɔ̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛ bo n'a̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧g, n a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yege, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
