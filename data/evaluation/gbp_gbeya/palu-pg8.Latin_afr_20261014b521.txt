Zɔfa ngɛm tɛ nɛ tɔk tɛ wireɛ.

Ṣ Go wese nɛ boō ńa̧ kpa tɔk a;
nɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔk tɛ owire
a ozgfa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛ bo nʼa̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧̧, na̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yege, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔk
kɔ mbe wirei, nɛ ̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
