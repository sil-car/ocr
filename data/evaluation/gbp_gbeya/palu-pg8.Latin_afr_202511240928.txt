Zɔ̧fa ngɛmı tɛ ȩ̧ nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wire.

Go wes mɛ̍ bo ᷄ ̧̧ kp̄a tɔ̧k na;,
ŋıs ganaa rɛmm a kurî na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozu̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛ bo nʼa̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ģ, n' a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yɛgɛ, bo ńa̧ si dhonģ, gʼ a̧ yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
