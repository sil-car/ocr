Zɔ̧fa ngɛm tɛ ȩ̧ nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wire.

̄ Go wese r̈ɛ b̄o ı̄ʼa̧ kṵpa tɔ̧k na;
ŋɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozɔ̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

G᷆o wese nɛ bo nʼa̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ, nʼ a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yɛgɛ, bo nʼ́a̧ si dong, gʼa̧ yɔng nmıa gere Wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
