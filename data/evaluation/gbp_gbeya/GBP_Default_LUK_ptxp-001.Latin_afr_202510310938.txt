Dedhe Nuûwen nɛ

LUKE
ɔvɔrɑ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhong̰waa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kriisti,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛtti kɔ
«Dɛ̂âmɔ̂dɛv kɔ̂ oBetom»› bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke vɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go vinaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼa̧ kidho wen zu dɛ̂âzan kɔ
Zezi nɛ̂ dhong̰waa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼa̧ vɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛâ wen kidhi owire
ŋɛ ɔwâ dhong̰waa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10)).
Nʼa̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem.. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ng̰â-
Sɔ̧̧̂dan dɛvâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛvâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛttii duro gan ombɛtti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nâng̰â ɔɔ mbɛtti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhong̰waa bûrô dhong owen
ŋnĝlɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
ŋɛ dɛvâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kuwɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛva zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fev
kʼaạ zu sang̰karân te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa kɛngbarâ tɛ ofev.

Tɛ sɔnkov mbɛttii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhong̰waa nɛ Zezi dung̰ɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̰ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa na̧ tunɔ ha tɛ̰
fevi. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhong̰waa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ Kʼɛ̰
wen usi Dedhe Nû̂wen ha owirei. Kɔraa na̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhong̰waa
ŋɛ Nzapa baa nɛ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
