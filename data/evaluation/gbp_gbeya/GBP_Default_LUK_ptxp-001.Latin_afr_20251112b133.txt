Dedhe Nûwen nɛ

LUKE
yɔrɑ̂ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ᷇ Luke

Wire nɛ yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaꞌa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmanɛg ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaꞌa kɔ᷇ Zezi-Kɑristi,
gan sɔn, Owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha yɔra mbɛti kɔ
«Dɛ̂yâmɔ᷇dɛy kɔ᷇ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke yɔrâi,
tɔwâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go yinaa ha Teyofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼạ kidho wen zu dɛ̂yâzan kɔ
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaꞌa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ̂ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ yɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ wire nɛ tɛyâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ yɔwâ dhongwaꞌa kʼôrô sidhayi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (l9: 10).
Nʼạ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô ģbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okoôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Sɔ̣᷇dan dɛyâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛyâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
yɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa yɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke kuwɔ nângâ yɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaꞌa bûrô dhong owen
ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu koy kɔ᷇ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛyâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛya zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼạ̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fey
kʼạ zu sang̰karânɛg te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼaạ̰ kɛngbarâ tɛ ofey.

Tɛ sɔnkoy mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaꞌa nɛ Zezi dunɛgɔ
kɔpa nɛ̂ nmaꞌa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼạ tunɔ ha tɛ
feyi. Luke tɔ̣wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼɛ̧
wen usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̣wâ wen zu dhongwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
