Zɔ̧fa ngɛmı tɛ nɛ tɔ̧k̍ tɛ wire.

~ Go wese mɛ Bo m᷄a̧ kpāa tɔ̧k na;
nıɛ ganaa rɛm a kurî na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a oz̧̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛ bo nʼa̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛg, nʼ a̧ kpa obedɔk~-zɛrɛi.
Zey yegɛ, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ !tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wrei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
