Dedhe Nuûwen nŋɛ

LUKE
yɔrɑ̂ nɛ mɓɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zl̂rɛ̂, ŋɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi̍ Dedhe Nuûwen nŋɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi̧
gan sɔɔnŋ, owire nŋɛ gan ɔ nŋɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Dɛ̂âmɔ̂dɛ̧ kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ t̂ kɔ̰ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke ̧ɔɔrâÂi, a̧
tɔ̰wâ wen ha ıma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go inaa ha Tȩofili̍.
Luke gan zɔɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̰ wantɛ nâÂâ, gʼa̧ kidho wen zu dɛ̂âzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nŋɛ ripʼrôi sokʼa̧ ɔra nŋɛ
mbɛti̍.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ ɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhaŋi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Na̧ usô Zezi tɛ zanŋ Ôôrô wire nŋɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nŋɛ owire zɔk wa̰
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nŋɛ Fɛ̂nŋgâ-
Sɔ̰ĵdan dɛŋâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛŋâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nuwen nŋɛ Luke
ɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔɔra kɔ̰ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi̍.

Luke kụwɔ nângâ ɔɔ mbɛti kʼ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûÊrô dhong o᷆wenŋ
ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu ko̧ kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼq̧ tɔ̰wâ wen zu onŋma mɔ
nɛ dɛ̧â mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kụwɔ nângâ tom kʼȩ̰ zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i̍. A tɔ̰wâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼa̧ tɔ̰wâ wen bhɔ zu fȩ
kʼa̧ zu sangkarânıg te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa̧ kɛngbarâ tɛ Ofȩ.

Tɛ sɔnŋko mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̰wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dunŋgɔ
kpa nɛ̂ ımaa in owiwemɔ kʼȩ̰ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa̧ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fevi. Luke tɔ̰wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼ̰̰
wen usi Dedhe Nuûûwen ha owirei̍. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̰wâ wen zu dhonŋgwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzanŋ mɛi.
