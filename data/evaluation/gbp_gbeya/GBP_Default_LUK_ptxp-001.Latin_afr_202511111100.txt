Dedhe Nûǣwen nꞋ

LUKE
yɛrẅ nꞋ mbꞋti

GꞋk wen zu mbꞋti kɛǣ Luke

Wire nꞋ yɛra mbꞋti nɛɓɛ ɛ nꞋ Luke. Â ɛ nꞋ gaɗsa wizam zꞋɓrꞋɓ, nꞋ
ganaa ɛ nꞋ Zuwif nâ. A yɛra mbꞋtiụ nɛɓɛ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nꞋ
haɗ kpasi kɛɓ nmang-nman̂ ha owire nꞋɓ dhonĝwaa kɛɓ Zezi-Kristi,
ĝan sɛn, owire nꞋ gan ɛ nꞋ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha yɛra mbꞋti kɛɓ
«̃DꞋɓyâmɛǣdꞋy kɛǣ oBetomɛ» bhɛ. NꞋ tꞋɓ kɛɗ ombꞋti riito nꞋ Luke yɛraâi, aɗ
tɛɗwâ wen ha nma wire nꞋ gan ɛ nꞋ Zuwif nâ go yinaa ha Teyofili.
Luke gan zɛk Zezi nꞋ rip ɗ wantꞋ nâ, gʼaɗ kidho wen zu dꞋɓyâzan kɛǣ
Zezi nꞋɓ dhonĝwaa âkâ owire nꞋ zɛkaâ Zezi nꞋ ripʼrôi sokʼƊɗ yɛra nꞋ
mbꞋtii.

Luke uso Zezi tꞋ zan ôrô Bem kɛǣ wire nꞋ tꞋyâ wen kidhi owire
nꞋ yɛwâ dhonĝwaa kʼôrô sidhayi ga wen zam kpasi kɛɓ wa (19:10).
Nʼaɗ usô Zezi tꞋ zan ôrô wire nꞋ zɛk ala kɛɓ owire nꞋ owire zɛk wa
ôrô ĝbɛlɛ wire gai: OwikɛlꞋ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nꞋ FꞋɓnĝâ-
Sɛɗɓdan dꞋyâ zan̂ kɛɓ omɛ nꞋ Zezi dꞋyâi bhɛ. Dedhe Nûwen nꞋ Luke
yɛrâ nꞋ mbꞋtii duro gan ombꞋti sɛn nꞋ wa yɛra kɛɗ Mbe-NdɛkꞋi.

Luke kuɗwɛ nânĝâ yɛɛ mbꞋti kʼꞋɗi nꞋ dhonĝwaa bûrô dhonĝ owen
ngꞋɓlꞋɓng-ngꞋɓlꞋɓnĝ zu koy kɛɓ Zezi, kɛraa nʼaɗ tɛɗwâ wen zu onma mɛ
nꞋ dꞋyâ mɛ tꞋɓ wese nꞋ Zezi kuɗwɛ nânĝâ tom kʼꞋɗ zû nu nɛǣɓi. A tɛɗwâ
wen dɛǣka zu omɛ nꞋ Zezi dꞋya zû nu nɛɓɓi, nʼaɗ tɛɗwâ wen bhɛ zu fey
kʼa zu sanĝkarân te in tûnɛɓǣwa kʼa kꞋngbarâ tꞋ ofey.

TꞋ sɛnkoy mbꞋtii, Luke tɛɗwâ wen zu dhonĝwaa nꞋ Zezi dunĝɛ
kpa nꞋɓ nmaa in owiwemɛ kʼꞋ̂ wââ dɛǣka-dɛǣka kɛraa nʼaɗ tunɛ ha tꞋɓ
feyi. Luke tɛɗwâ wen bhɛ zu dhonĝwaa nꞋ Zezi dhafa owiwemɛ KʼʼꞋ̂
wen usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɛraa nʼaɗ tɛɗwâ wen zu dhonĝwaa
nꞋ Nzapa baa nꞋɓ Zezi tꞋ ripzan mꞋi.
