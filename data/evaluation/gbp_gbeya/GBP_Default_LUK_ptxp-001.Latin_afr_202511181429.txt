Dɔedhe Nûwen nɛ

LUKE
ɔrɑ̂ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A vɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Ɗf̂âmɔ᷇dɛv kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke vɔrâ, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go vinaa ha Tevofli.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ȩ wantɛ nâ, gʼa kidho wen zu dḛ̂vâzan kɔ᷇
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ vɔra nɛ
mbɛti.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ᷇ wire nɛ tɛvâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ vɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼạ usoô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ F̂ngâ-
Sɔ̧dan dɛâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ ɔɔ mbɛti kʼi nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ngḛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngḛ̂lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ Zezi,̧ kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kụu̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼ zû nu nɔ᷇ɔi. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ᷇ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛva zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼạ̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fev
kʼạ zu sangkarâng te in tûmɔ̂wa kʼa kɛngbarâ tɛ ofev.

Tɛ sɔnko mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nḛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ wââ dɔ᷇ka-dɔ᷇ka kɔraa nʼạ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fevi. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ Kʼ
wen usi Dedhe Nû᷇wen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
