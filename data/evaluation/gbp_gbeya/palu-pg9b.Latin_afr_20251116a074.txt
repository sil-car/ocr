., Koɔɔ nɔwa vina nɛ wizamı-zɛrɛ ha̧a̧ hʼa̧,
nımaꞌa nɛɛ zɛrɛi gan kɔ ķ̍kutiva; ga̧ ̧v, f̌
nıɛ we tɛ a̧ gan ga̧ na.

go tɛ a̧ na̧k oro wire nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ fivo-bịri̍ ga,
