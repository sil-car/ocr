Dedhe NûẄwen nɑ

LUKE
ꞌrʉ nɑ mbɑti

Gɑk wen zu mbɑti kꞌ̰ Luke

Wire nɑ vꞌra mbɑti nꞌḛẄꞌ ꞌ nɑ Luke. A ꞌ nɑ gẍsa wizam zêẄrẂẄ, nɑ
ganaa ꞌ nɑ ZuwWif nâ. A vꞌra mbɑti nꞌẄꞌ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɑ
hẍ̄ kpasi kǣꞌẄ nmang-nmang ha owire nɑẂẄ dhongwaa kꞌ̰ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sꞌn, oWire nɑ gan ꞌ nɑ oZuwWif nâi. uke ha ꞌra mbɑti kꞌẄ
«DẂẄâmꞌ̰dɑv kꞌ̰ oBetom» bhꞌ. Nɑ tẂẄ kꞌ̄ ombɑti riito nɑ Luke vꞌrâi, ẍ
tꞌ̄wâẄ wen ha nma wire nɑ gan ꞌ nɑ ZuWif nâ go vinaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zꞌk Zezi nɑ rip ̄ wantɑ nâ, gʼa kidho wen zu dẂẄâzan kꞌ̰
Zezi nẂẄ dhongwaa âkâẄ owire nɑ zꞌkâẄ Zezi nɑ ripʼrôi sokʼaẍ vꞌra nɑ
mnbɑtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɑ zan ôrô Bem kꞌ̰ wire nɑ tɑâ wen kidhi owire
nɑ ꞌwâẄ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam kpasi kꞌẄ wa (19:10).
Nʼẍ usô Zezi tɑ zan ôrô wire nɑ zꞌk ala kǣꞌ̰ owire nɑ owire zꞌk wa
ôrô gbꞌlꞌ wire gai: Owikꞌlɑ, okô̰o in obem. Luke usô tom nɑ FɑẄngâ-
S̄dan dɑâ zang kꞌẄ omꞌ nɑ Zezi dɑvâi bhꞌ. Dedhe NûẄwen nɑ Luke
vꞌrâ nɑ mbɑtii duro gan ombɑti sꞌn nɑ wa ꞌra kꞌ̄ Mbe-Ndꞌkɑi.

Luke kẑ̄wꞌ nângƐâ ꞌꞌ mbɑti kʼɑ̄i nɑ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ngẄlẂẄng-ngɑêẄlêẄng zu kov kꞌ̰ Zezi, kꞌraa nʼaẍ tꞌ̄wâẄ wen zu onma mꞌ
nɑ dɑvâẄ mꞌ tẂẄ wese nɑ Zezi kẑ̄wꞌ nângâẄ tom kʼɑ̄ zûẄ nu nḛ̰Ẅꞌ̰i. A tꞌ̄wâẄ
wven dꞌ̰ka zu omꞌ nɑ Zezi dɑva zû nu nḛẄꞌ̰i, nʼẍ tꞌ̄wâẄ wen bhꞌ zu fe
kʼẍ zu sangkarâng te in tûnꞌḛẄwa kʼa kɑngbarâ tɑ ofev.

Tɑ sꞌnkov mbɑtii, Luke tꞌ̄wâẄ wen zu dhongwaa nɑ Zezi dungꞌ
kpa nɑẂẄ nmaa in owiwemꞌ kʼɑƐ wââ dꞌẄka-dꞌ̰ka kꞌraa nʼa tunꞌ ha tɑẄ
fevi. Luke tꞌ̄wâẄ wen bhꞌ zu dhongwaa nɑ Zezi dhafa owiwemꞌ kʼɑƐ
wven usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kꞌraa nʼẍ̄ tꞌ̄wâẄ wen zu dhongwaa
nɑ Nzapa baa nẂẄ Zezi tɑ ripzan mɑi.
