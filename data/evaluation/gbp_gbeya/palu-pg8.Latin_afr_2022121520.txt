Z̧fa ngem tɛ ȩ̧ nɛ tgk tɛ wire.

ʼ o wese ̍ɛb̃o̧᷄ kpa ţkna;
nɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tgk tɛ owire
a oz̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

G᷆o wese nɛbo nʼ̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ, nʼa̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yege, bo nʼq̧ si dhong, gʼ̧ yong nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tgk
kɔ mbe wirei, nɛ ̧ bhɔ ̧̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
