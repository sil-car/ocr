Zɔ̧fa nɛmı tɛ ɛ̧ nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wıire.

~ ʼŒGo wse mɛ ɓo m̄ʼa̧ Kk tɔ̧k na;
nıɛ ganaa rɛm a kurî na.

Nɛ dhonģwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a oz̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

G᷆o wese nɛ bo nʼ a̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ, nıʼ a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yɛge, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere Wire,
nɛ oɓbedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaa᷇i a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
