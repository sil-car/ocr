Zɔ̧fa nɑɛmı tɛ ģ nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wire.

ŒGo wese mɛ bo᷇ m᷄ a̧ kpa tɔ̧k na;,
nıɛ ganaa rɛm a kui na.

Nɛ dhonɑ̰waa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozɔ̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛ bo nʼa̧ vɔng nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ģ, n' a̧ kpa obedɔk~zɛrɛi.
Zȩ vegɛ, bo nʼa̧ si dɨonģ, gʼ a̧ vɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ va zangaai a tɛ ţk
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ æ̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
