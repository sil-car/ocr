LUKE
ɔr nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ᷇ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ᷇ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wWizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A ɔra mıbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ᷇ nmang-nman ha owire nɛ̂ dhonwaa kɔ᷇ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ᷇
«Dɛ̂vâmɔ᷇dɛv kɔ᷇ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke vɔrâi, aạ̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go vinaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ȩ wantɛ nâ, gʼa kidho wen zu dɛ̂vâzan kɔ᷇
Zezi nɛ̂ dhonwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ̧ vɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ᷇ wire nɛ tɛvâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ ɔwâ dhonwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ᷇ wa (1 9:10).
Nʼa̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ᷇ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okoôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Sɔ̧dan dɛâ zang kɔ᷇ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mıbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ ɔɔ mbɛti kʼi nɛ dhongwaa burô dhon owen
ngḛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngḛ̂lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kụwɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ zû nu nɔ᷇ɔ᷇i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ᷇ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛva zû nu nɔ᷇ɔ᷇i, nʼạ̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fe
kʼạ̧ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ᷇wa kʼa kɛngbarâ tɛ ofe.

Tɛ sɔnko mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nḛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ wââ dɔ᷇ka-dɔ᷇ka kɔraa nʼạ̧ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ Kʼɛ
wven usi Dedhe NẄwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼạ̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhonwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
