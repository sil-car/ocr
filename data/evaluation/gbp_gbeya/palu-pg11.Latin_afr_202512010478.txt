Ɔɔ ro̧k ɛrɛ zey rıɛʼrɛ dɛ omɔ nɛ wiza᷇m᷅ı-zɛrɛ
tɔ̧ h̀aʼrɛ nɛ dhcngwvɛra, zey yege,
bo“rɛ kɔ-d̍cmga ha zɛrɛ kʼɛrɛ sɔn̄ l̍ãrn.-

Wi nıgɛm tɛʼi wenkɔ diiorn̄gwaa rowvæ
yi̍na zɛrɛ gɛ̧g Ɔ zang-zahg:
kɔ osɔk̀a Wire Ɔ Ɛɛnɛ,
hɨɛ kɔ obemn ɔ sɛnɛ bɓɔḧ̍ıɔ.
Nɛ cnuwey zɛrɛ gɛ̧s yoc d̍ɔk.
Zḛy yege, zɛrɛ gɛ̧gi bo ɔ ngay go wizɛrɛi Q̧y,
nɛɛ a kin mɛ ha̧ yina h'a̧ nɛ tan mɛ ra.
Ms nɛ tɛ wizam-zɛrɛ, nıɛ nʼa̧ t; muɔra h́a mɛ!
