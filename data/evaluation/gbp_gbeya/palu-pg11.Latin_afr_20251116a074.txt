Ɔ᷇ rɔo̧k ɛrɛ ze rıɛ́rɛ d̍ɛ omnɔ nɛ wizamı-zɛrɛ
tɔ̧ haʼrɛ nɛ dhœnwwara̧, ze ege,
bo“rɛ ɔ-dc̄ıga h̍ıa :ɛrɛ k“ɛrɛ sɔn !̍amn.

Wi ngɛm tɛʼi wenkɔ diongwaa᷇ nɔwva
i̍na zɛfɛ gɛ̧Ȩ̧ ɔ zan-zan:
kɔ osɔka Wire Ɔ Ɛɛnɛ,
hɛɛ kɔ obem ɔ sɛnɛ bh̍ıɔ.
Nɛ cnuwe zɛrɛ gɛ̧ voc d̍ɔk.
Zɛ veg, zɛrɛ gɛ̧;i bo᷇ ɔ nga go wWizɛrɛli ̧,
nɛɛ a kịn mɛɛ ha̧ vina h'a̧ nɛ tan mɛɛ r:a.
Ms nɛ tɛ wizam-zɛrɛ, nɛ nʼa̧ tɔ̧ mɔra ha mmɛ!
