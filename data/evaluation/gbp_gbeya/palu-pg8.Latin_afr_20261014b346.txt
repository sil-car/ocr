Zgfa ngɛm tɛ ɛ nɛ tk tɛ wire.

Ṣ Go wese nɛ bo ńa̧ kpa tɔ̧k rīa;
nɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a oz̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛ bo nʼa̧ yƴɔng nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧̧, nʼa̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yege, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔk
kɔ mbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
