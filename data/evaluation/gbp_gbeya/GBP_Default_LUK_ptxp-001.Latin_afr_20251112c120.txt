Dedhe Nuûûwen nŋɛ

LUKE
Xɔrɑ̂ nɛ mɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛl̂rɛÊ̂, nŋɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe NuûÊwen nŋɛ
ha̰ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛl̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi̍-K᷄risti̧
gan sɔɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Dɛ̂âmɔ̂dɛ kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tl̂ kɔ̰ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke ̧ɔrâÂi, a̧
tɔ̰wâ wen ha ıma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go inaa ha TȩOfili̍.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̰ wantɛ nâÂâ, gʼa̧ kidho wen zu dɛl̂âzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrÔôi sokʼa̧ ɔra nŋɛ
mbɛti̍.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan Ôôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ ɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhai ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼa̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan Ôôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂nŋgâ-
Sɔ̂ĵdan dɛŋâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛŋâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ Luke
ɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̰ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ ɔɔ mbɛti kʼi nɛ dhongwaa bûÊrô dhong owenŋ
ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛl̂ng zu koŋ kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼq̧ tɔ̰wâ wen zu onŋma mɔ
nɛ dɛâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kuụwɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i̍. A tɔ̰wâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼa̧ tɔ̰wâ wen bhɔ zu fȩ
kʼa̧ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa kɛngbarâ tɛ Ofȩ.

Tɛ sɔnkoy mbɛtii̍, Luke tɔ̰wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dunŋgɔ
kpa nɛ̂ ımaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̰ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa̧ tunɔ ha tĝ̂
fevi. Luke tɔ̰wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ ķʼ̰̰
wen usi Dedhe Nuûwen ha owirei̍. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̰wâ wen zu dhongwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛl̂ Zezi tɛ ri̍pzanŋ mɛi.
