Zɔ̧fa ngɛm tɛ ȩ̧ nɛ tɔ̧k̍ tɛ wire.

Go wese rɛ b̄o ᷄ʼ a̧ kpa tɔ̧k na;
nıɛ ganaa rɛm a kurị na.

Nɛ dhohgwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozɔ̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Gìo wese nɛ bo nʼ a̧ yɔng nıma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ, nʼ a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yege, bo n'ʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nıma gere Wire,
nɛɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧̧k
kɔ mıbe wire¡, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛꞌi.
