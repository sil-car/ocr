Zɔ̧fa nɛm tɛ ģ nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wire.

Go ws rɛ bo m̃ʼ a̧ ka tɔ̧k na,
ŋɨɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri Ŋa.

Nɛ dhonɑ̧waa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozɔ̧fa a nɛɛ kuri kʼoro.

Gɨ᷆o wese nɛ bo ņ yɔng nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ̧, nʼ a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey ygɛ, bo nʼ̧ si donģ, gʼ a̧ yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wirei, nɛ æ̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
