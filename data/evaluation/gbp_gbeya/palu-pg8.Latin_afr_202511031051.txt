Zɔ̧fa nɛm tɛ ȩ̧ nɛ tɔ̧k̍ tɛ wire.

~ Go wese mɛ o ᷄ʼa̧ kṵā tɔ̧k na;
ŋıɛ ganaa rɛm a kurî na.

Nɛ dhonɑ̧waa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozɔ̧fa a nɛɛ kuri kʼoro..

G᷆o wese nɛ bo na̧ yɔnɑg nma wire
nɛ Ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ, nʼ a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛ.
Zey yegɛ, bo nʼa̧ si dhonɑ̰, gʼa̧ yɔnɑ nma gere Wire,
nɛɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zang̰aai a tɛ Ɨɔ̧k
kɔ mıbe wire, nɛ æ̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
