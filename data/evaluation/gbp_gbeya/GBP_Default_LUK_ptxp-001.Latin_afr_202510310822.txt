Dedhe Nû̂wen nɛ

LUKE
ɔvɔrɑ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A ɔra mbbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nnang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, Oowire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbbɛti kɔ
«Dɛ̂âmɔ̂dɛv kɔ̂ oBetom»› bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ omɛti riito nɛ Luke ɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nmna wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go vinaa ha Tevofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼạ kidho wen zu dɛ̂âzan kɔ
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼa ɔra nɛ
mɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ ɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhavi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10)).
Nʼa usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem.. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Sɔ̧̧̂dan dɛâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
vɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nâng̰â ɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu kov kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼa tɔ̧wâ wen zu onmna mɔ
nɛ dɛâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂ɔi. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhhɔ zu fev
kʼạ zu sangkarân te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa kɛngbarâ tɛ ofev.

Tɛ sɔnko mɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa n̂ naa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̰ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼʼɛ
wen usi Dedhe Nû̂wen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa
ŋɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
