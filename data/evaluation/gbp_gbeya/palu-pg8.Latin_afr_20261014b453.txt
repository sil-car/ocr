Zɔfa ngɛm tɛ nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wire.

Ṣ Go wese nɛ bö ɨa̧ kpa tɔk ra;
nɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a oz̧fa a nɛ kuri kʼoro.

Go wese nɛĴ bo nʼʉ̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ̧, nʼa̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yege, bo nʼa̧ si dhong, gʼa̧ yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛĴ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔk
kɔ mbe wirei, nɛ ̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
