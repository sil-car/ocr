Ɔ᷇› rɔo̧k ɛrɛ zey rıɛʼrɛ dɛ omɔ nɛ wi̍zamı-zɛrɛ
tɔ̧ haʼrɛ nɛ dhonʉɛra, zey yege,
bo“rɛ k̍ɔ-d̀hoŋga hha zɛrɛ k'́ɛrɛ sɔn l̍arn.-

Wi nıgɛmm tɛʼi wenkɔ doŋwaa nwva
yi̍ma zɛfɛ ģ̧ Ɔ zang-z̈ang:
kɔ osɔka Wire Ɔ Ɛɛn,
hʉɛ kɔ obemmı Ɔ sɛnɛ bh̍ıƆ.
Nɛ onuwey zɛrɛ gɛ̧g yo d̍ɔk.
Z y yeɛ, zɛrɛ gɛ̧̧gi bo ɔ ngay go Wizɛrɛi Ɔy,
nɛ a kịn mɛ ha̧ yina h'a̧ nɛ tan mıɛ ra.
Mɛ nɛ tɛ wizam-zɛrɛ, nıɛ nʼa̧ tɔ̧ mɔra h́a mɛ!
