nɛn niye desmalꞋ, de zaa tarə manꞋ. Niye desmalꞋ
ma bhoro yoĝe tꞋ: ““ Dhhi usi gṵdi ma tu”, na yoge eĝi
zaĝu kovu nzili tꞋ: ““ Kori kal rhubha, de zaa, zi ze
ĝara ZꞋrə, ze zʼas kusu ĝidi de zaa gunu”ʼ. Yoĝe uni
dhovo deri nꞋ. Kazə nɛn, Kokudho wo vɛyɛ nĝono
