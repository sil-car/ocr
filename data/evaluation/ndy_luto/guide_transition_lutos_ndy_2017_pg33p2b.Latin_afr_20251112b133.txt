Kidhe na ngono manda gḛe ha vaka ma̧, yoge emvi
nɔn niye desmalɛ, de zaꞌa tarə manɛ. Niye desmalɛ
ma bhoro yogḛe tɛ: ““ Dɔhi usi gidi ma tu”ʼ, na yoge egi
zagu kovu nzili tɛ: ““ Kori kal rhubha, de zaꞌa, zi ze
gara zɛrə, ze zʼas kusu gidi de zaꞌa gunu”ʼ. Yogḛe uni
dhovo deri nɛ. Kazə nɔn, Kokudho wo vɔyɔ ngono
