Kidhe na nɛg̰ono manda gḛ ha vaka ma, yoge emvi
nɔn niye desmalɛ, de zaꞌa tarə manɛ. Niye desmalɛ
ma bhoro yogḛ tɛ: ““ Dhhi usi gidi ma tuʼ”ʼ, na yogḛ egƁi
zagṵu kovu nzili tɛ: ““ Kori kal rhubha, de zaꞌa, zi ze
g̰ara Zɛrə, Ze zʼas kusu gidi de zaꞌa gunu”ʼ. Yoge uni
dhovo deri nɛ. Kazə nɔn, Kokudho wo vɔyɔ ngono
