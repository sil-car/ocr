nɔn niye desmalɛ, de zaa tarə manɛ. Niye desmalɛ
ma bhoro yogḛ tɛ: “ Dhi usi gidi ma tu”ʼ, na yogḛ egi
zagu kovu nzili tɛ: “ Kori kal rhubha, de zaa, zi ze
gara zɛrə, Zze zʼ as kusu gidi de zaa gunu”ʼ. Yogḛ uni
dhovo deri nɛ. Kaazə nɔn, Kokudho wo vɔyɔ ngono
