nɔn niye desmalɛ, de zaa tarə manɛ. Niye desmalɛ
ma bhoro yoge tɛ: ““ Dɔhi usi gidi ma tuʼʼ, na yogḛe egi
zagu kowu nzili tɛ: ““ Kori kal rhubha, de zaa, zi ze
gara Zɛrə, Ze Zʼas kusu gidi de zaa gunu”ʼ. Yogḛe uni
dhovo derri nɛ. Kazə nɔn, Kokudho wo vɔyɔ ngono
