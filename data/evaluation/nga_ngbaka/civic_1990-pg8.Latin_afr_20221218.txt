3. To nwa kɛ ma d̄o ngɔ gi1i partɨ
po1itɨque wena  (Mu1ti-partisme)

Kɔə  to nwa ki ni, owií kɔ parti po1itique kɛ
wa saka wa ni wa e mbu1a. Ha ɛa 1ɨ  tọ nwa
ki  ní na 1a démocratie. 0wɨ kɔ 1enɔ vɛ́ wa
wia̍ we  1e  kɔ  parti  po1it̍ique. Nɛ  ꞌd̃ɑ
sabɛ1ɛ na1ɛ naɛ, tabɨ sabɛ1ɛ mɔ1ɔ mɔ1ɔ̣, nɛ
wa saka sanga oparti po1ítique we  kp̍a-̍mbé
owi  e mbu1anɔ.  Parti kɛ ma nɛ ngɔ ngbɛ
n̍ɛ nwa ngɔ ma  hɔ P̄remier Ministre. Partɨ
ki  nɨ̣ a d̃ɛ sɛ to nwa ꞌd sabɛ1ɛ 4 tabɨ 5,
nɛ wa d᷆̄ɛ mbɛ́ vɔtɛ.  Fa1a kɛ parti kɨni d̄ɛ
toe ɔ d́ia, nɛ wa kõ̃ na a d̄ɛ ꞌbɔ mbé́ nɛ.  Kɔ
n̍ɨ gɔ, nɛ wa sɛaka mbé́ partɨ.

Ïɛ̰!ɛ t̍ɛ part̍inɔ

Partɨ po1itique ma kpɨ d᷇  kpɨ,  si  ngɔ
inggamɔ kɛ wa wia we d̃ɛ to mbati ngɔ nı̀i
n̍i.  Owɨnɔ wa a ngɔ gi1ɨ̍partinɔ nɨ ɔ nɛ ma
n̍gɔ  fɛ1ɛ  ni, vo1o ga1e we hɔ nga wɨ1i k5.
Nɛ wa sa 1ií nɛ  na: part̍ɨ gauche (si ga1e)
tabɨ parti droite (nga wɨ1i kə̃), tabi parti
kɛ ma d̄ɛ gbogbo nɨ.  Lɛ zɔ tɛ1ɛ tɛ 1inggamɛ
ꞌda̍ opartinɔ n.

Parti gauche: ẅa 1engge na, gouvernement̍ hã
zɔ mɔ tɛ mɛnɔ vɛ̌ kɛ winɔ̣wa  d́ɛ n, t̍akɔ
owinɔ  vƐ̍  wa d̃ungu ɔ d̄ía, nɛ wa ɔ d́o kpasi
kp̍o.  Extreme gauche, mɛ a parti Communiste
(kɔ Russie ...) Tɛ1ɛ tɛ parti kɛ  gɛ ma ks̃a
n̍a owinɔ vɛ́ wa d̃ɛ to fa1a nɛ kpo tɛ kɔ  gʉ
fɔnɔ,  nɛ  we1e  tɛ 1eè᷅nggè do fɔɛ ꞌda a iko
gɔ.
