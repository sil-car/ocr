3. Mɛ kɛ cḭtoven wia we d̄ɛ
(Les d᷆evoirs d̄u cịtoven)

1. Ma wia na be Zaire d̀ɛ m̄bṵla ngɔ nɑ̀i ꞌd̀a
a, nɛ a d̄ɔnggɔ onwa ngɔ le ꞌd̀a a.

Mbe ombɑlanɔ kɔ le ꞌd̃a lɛ:

Ma wia na obe Zaire vɛ̃, wanɔ̍wa wḭa sabɛlɛ
tako ni, wa futatakoe ɔ nɛ kɛ mbula ꞌd̃a lɛ
be ma ni.

Ma w̄ia na be Zaire vɛ̃ ɔ ɑ̃o kartɛ citoven.
Nɛ ma wè᷅ na a ele m̄a kɔ́ vali ꞌda̍a gɔ.
Ɨala kɛ wa ba mɔ, nɛ nde mɔ d̄o kartɛ bina,
wa d᷆ɛ sɛ fala ẑ̃ na, mɔ a be Zaire ni nɛ

ẑ2. Ma wia na be Zaire d̍ɛ mɔ d̄o wala olo nɛ

Ngangga gɛ, lɛ̄ zɔ̍a na wɔ m̀bil1i mã gã kɔ
əsila winɔ wena. Takɔ na wa d̀ɛ tonɔ̍̈ꞌda wã
d̃o wala o̰lo nɛ ̣ni, nɛ wa kŊ̂a na wele hʉ̃
nmbili hʉ lo, sɛ nɛ̣̀lo̍ d̍ɛ to hã a d̍ḛ.- Na mɛ
n̄ɨ, wi sʉnɔ wã kp̄a zɛlɛ tɛ wena, nɛ wɨ
mbilinɔ wa wḭạ we d̀ɛ mɛnɔ vɛ̃ zã wa kɔ9a ni
d̄o m̄bɨlɨ ̍ꞌd̄ã ẅ̀a. Ki ̍ni ʼmaā ꞌdanga le
ꞌd̄anga. ʉ

Ma wia na, lɛ kunu ɛila lɛ d̄atịi mbili, lɛ
tɛ d̄è to tabi gbɛ d̄ia gulu we hʉ̃ wele we kɛ
lɛ kpa m̄bili kɔ a, tabi we kɛ lɛ ́ ti a
nɨ ḡə- Lɛ d̄ɛ nı̰̰᷇ nd̄e lɛ ꞌdanga le ꞌda lɛ
ꞌdanga.
