po1ɨtɨque wena  (Mu1t̍ɨ-partɨsme)

wa saka wa nɨ wa e nbu1a. Wa sa 1ɨ  to  nwa
kɨ  nɨ na 1a de᷇mocratɨe.  O0wɨ kɔ 1enɔ vɛ wa
wɨa we  1e  kɔ  partɨ  po1ɨt̍ique. Ne  ꞌd̍o
sabɛ1ɛ na1ɛ na1ɛ, tabɨ sabɛ1ɛ mnɔ1ɔ mnɔ1ɔ̰, nɛ
wa saka sanga opartɨ po1ɨt̍ɨque we  kp̍a -mbé
owɨ  e mb̍u1anɔ.  Partɨ kɛ ma nɛ ngɔ ngbɛ ɛ,
nɛ nwa ngɔ ma  hɔ P᷆remnɨer Mɨnistre. Partɨ
kɨ  nɨ́  a d̃ɛ sɛ to nwa ꞌd̈̍o sabɛ1ɛ 4  tabɨ 5,
nɛ wa d᷆ɛ mbé vɔtɛ,  Fa1a kɛ partɨ kɨ nɨ  dɛ

nɨ gɔ, neɛ wa saka mbeé partɨ.
