3. To nwa kɛ ma do ngɔ gili partḭ
polit ɨque wena (Multi~partisme )

Kɔ to nwa ki ni, owi kɔ parti politique kɛ
wa saka wa ni wa e mbula. Wa ɛa li to nwa
ki ni na la deé᷇mocratḭe. Owi kɔ lenɔ vƐ̍ wa
wia we le kɔ parti poli̍tique. Nɛ - ꞌdo
sabɛlɛ nalɛ nalɛ, tabi sabɛlɛ mɔlɔ mɔlɔ, nɛ
wa saka sanga opartḭ politique we kpa -ʼmbé
owi e mbulanɔ. Partḭ kɛ ma nɛ ngɔ ngbɛ ɛ,
nɛ nwa ngɔ ma hɔ Premier Ministre. Part
ki nı a d᷆ɛ sɛ to nwa ꞌdo sabɛlɛ 4 tabı᷄ 5,
nɛ wa d̀ɛ mb᷄ vɔtɛ. Fala kɛ parti ki ʼ̄ni dɛ
ftoe ɔ d̄ḭ̍a, nɛ wa k̃ na a dɛ ꞌbɔ mbé nɛ. Kɔ
nıi gɔ, nɛ wa saka mbé part̍.
