Tɑ᷅talɨ̍ma ᷅naɑ̰hën

Wa moô wɨ̈lɨgɨ̈ mbɨ̈tɨ mê̂ kɑ̧ ̈l̂ geê̂ge mb̈tɨ̍ nɑ̈a gbɑ̈gba mb̈tɨ̈ nŋâ

ngba᷅ të nza᷅kɑ̈lɑ᷅ o, wa më

1) Mo le᷅lëng̰ba᷆ bɑ̈ g̰bâ mb̈tɨ᷅ nâ ngbä nŋzɑ̈k̈lɑ̈ bɑ̈ tɨ̈mɑ nɨ̈ mʉ̈
kâ mʉ̈ la᷅, n̂ Bakumɑ̰, Gambu waɑ̧lɑ̰ Banŋgasu. Moô mâ ba᷇ndɨɨ
lu᷅mo᷅ nɑ᷅a mvogoô s̈ pë, ÿ ga të̈ ka bâ̂tɨ gbɑ̈ gbɑa mb̈tɨ̈ Zagi
kaa ngb̈ ŋ̧zɑ̰kɑ̰lɑ̧

2) Moô gb̂ bɑạnŋdɨ̣ pɑnŋdɨɨ n̂ ngbɑ̈ të̈ nza̰kalɑ̰
