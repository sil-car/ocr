Dedhe Nûwen nꞋ

LUkKE
yɛrẅ nꞋ mbꞋti

GꞋk wen zu mbꞋti kɛǣ Luke

Wire nꞋ yɛra mbꞋti nɛɓɛ ɛ nꞋ Luke. A ɛ nꞋ gaɗsa wizam zꞋɓrꞋɓ, nꞋ
ganaa ɛ nꞋ Zuwif nâ. A yɛra mbꞋti nɛɓɛ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nꞋ
haɗ kpasi kɛɓ nmang-nman ha owire nꞋɓ dhongwaa kɛ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɛn, owire nꞋ gan ɛ nꞋ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha yɛra mbꞋti kɛɓ
«DꞋɓyâmɛǣdꞋy kɛǣ oBetomɛ» bhɛ. NꞋ tꞋɓ kɛɗ ombꞋti riito nꞋ Luke yɛraâi, aɗ
tɛɗwâ wen ha nma wire nꞋ gan ɛ nꞋ Zuwif nâ go yinaa ha Teyofili.
Luke gan zɛk Zezi nꞋ rip Ꞌɗ wantꞋ nâ, gʼa kidho wen zu dꞋɓyâzan kɛ
Zezi nꞋɓ dhongwaa âkâ owire nꞋ zɛkâ Zezi nꞋ ripʼrôi sokʼaɗ yɛra nꞋ
mıbꞋtii.

Luke uso Zezi tꞋ zan ôrô Bem kɛɓ wire nꞋ tꞋyâ wen kidhi owire
nꞋ yɛwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhayi ga wen zam kpasi kɛɓ wa (19: 10).
Nʼaɗ usô Zezi tꞋ zan ôrô wire nꞋ zɛk ala kɛɓ owire nꞋ owire zɛk wa
ôrô gbɛlɛ wire gai: OwikɛlꞋ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nꞋ FꞋɓnâ-
Sɛɗɗɓdan dꞋyâ zang kɛɓ omɛ nꞋ Zezi dꞋyâi bhɛ. Dedhe Nûwen nꞋ Luke
yɛrâ nꞋ mbꞋtii duro gan ombꞋti sɛn nꞋ wa yɛra kɛɗ Mbe-NdɛkꞋi.

Luke kuɗwɛ nânâ yɛɛ mbꞋti kʼꞋɗi nꞋ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ngꞋɓlꞋɓn-ngꞋɓlꞋɓng zu koy kɛɓ Zezi, kɛraa nʼaɗ tɛɗwâ wen zu onma mɛ
nꞋ dꞋyâ mɛ tꞋɓ wese nꞋ Zezi kuɗwɛ nângâ tom kʼꞋɗ zû nu nɛɓɛǣi. A tɛɗwâ
wen dɛǣka zu omɛ nꞋ Zezi dꞋya zû nu nɛɓɛɓi, nʼaɗ tɛɗwâ wen bhɛ zu fey
kʼaɗ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɛɓwa kʼa kꞋngbaraâ tꞋ ofey.

TꞋ sɛnkoy mbꞋtii, Luke tɛɗwâ wen zu dhongwaa nꞋ Zezi dungɛ
kpa nꞋ‹ɓ nmaa in owiwemɛ kʼꞋɗ wââ dɛɓka-dɛǣka kɛraa nʼaɗ tunɛ ha tꞋ
feyi. Luke tɛɗwâ wen bhɛ zu dhongwaa nꞋ Zezi dhafa owiwemɛ kKʼʼꞋ
wen usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɛraa nʼaɗ tɛɗwâ wen zu dhongwaa
nꞋ Nzapa baa nꞋɓ Zezi tꞋ ripzan mꞋi.
