Dedhe Nûwen nɛ

LUKE
yɔrɑ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaꞌa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhong̰waꞌa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha yɔra mbɛti kɔ
«Dɛ̂yâmɔ᷇dɛy kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke yɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nıma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go yinaꞌa ha Teyofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼa kidho wen zu dɛ̂yâzan kɔ
Zezi nɛ̂ dhong̰waꞌa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼa̧ yɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ wire nɛ tɛyâ wen kidhi owire
ŋɛ yɔwâ dhong̰waꞌa kʼôrô sidhayi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼa̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okoo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Ş̧dan dɛyâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛyâꞌi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
yɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa yɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nâng̰â yɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaꞌa bûrô dhong owen
ŋngɛ̂lɛ̂n̰-ngɛ̂lɛ̂n̰ zu koy kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraꞌa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛyâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zûl nu nɔ̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛya zû nu nɔ̂ɔi, nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fey
kʼa̧ zu sangkkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa̧ kɛngbarâ tɛ ofey.

Tɛ sɔnkoy mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaꞌa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kɔpa nɛ̂ nmaꞌa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̣ wâꞌâ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraꞌa nʼa̧ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
feyi. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaꞌa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ Kʼʼɛ
wen usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraꞌa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaꞌa
ŋɛ Nzapa baꞌa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
