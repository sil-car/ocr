Dedhe Nûwen nɛ

LUKE
yɔrâ nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ᷇ Luke

Wire nɛ yɔra mbɛti nɔ᷇ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛrɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kɛpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmanɛ ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ᷇ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha yɔra mbɛti kɔ
«Dɛ̂yâmmɔ᷇dɛy kɔ᷇ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke yɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nıma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go yinaa ha Teyofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼạ kidho wen zu dɛ̂yâzan kɔ
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ̧ yɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ᷇ wire nɛ tɛyâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ yɔwâ dhong̰waa kʼôrô sidhayi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼa̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ᷇ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem.. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Ş̧dan dɛyâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛyâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
yɔrâl nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa yɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ yɔɔ mbɛti kʼ ɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ngɛlɛ̂ng-ngɛlɛng zu koy kɔ᷇ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛyâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ᷇ɔ᷇i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ᷇ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛya zû nu nɔ̂ɔ᷇i, nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâl wen bhɔ zu fey
kʼạ zu sang̰karân te in tûnɔwa kʼa kɛngbarâ tɛ ofey.

Tɛɛ sɔnkoy mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ wââ dɔka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa̰ tunɔ ha tɛ̂
feyi. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ Kkʼɛ̰
wen usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
