Dedhe Nuwen nɛ

LUKE
yɔrâ nɛ mbɛti

Gɨɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛrɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang-nmang ha owire nɛ̂ dhongwaa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha yɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Dɛyâmɔ̂dɛy kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke yɔrâi, ạ
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go yinaa ha Teyofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ȩ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼạ kidho wen zu dɛ̂yâzan kɔ̂
Zezi nɛ̂ dhongwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼạ yɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛyâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ yɔwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhayi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10).
Nʼa usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Ş̧dan dɛyâ zang kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛyâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
yɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa yɔra ķ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nângâ yɔɔ mbɛti k'ʼgi nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhong owen
ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu koy kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛyâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i. A ţwâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛya zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼạ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fey
kʼạ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa kɛngbarâ tɛ ofey.

Tɛ sɔnkoy mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa tunɔ ha tɛ̂
feyi. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼg
wen usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa ţwâ wen zu dhongwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
