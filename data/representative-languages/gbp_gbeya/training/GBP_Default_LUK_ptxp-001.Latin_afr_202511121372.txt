DŝDedhe Nuûûwen nŋ

LUKE
yɑr̄ nŋ mbŋti

Gŋk wen zu mbŋti kɑ̄ Luke

Wire nŋ yɑra mbŋti nɑ̄ɑ ɑ nŋ Luke. A ɑ nŋ gaɨsa wizam z̄rŋ̄, nŋ
ganaa ɑ nŋ Zuwif nâ. A yɑra mbŋti nɑ̄ɑ wen usi Dedhe Nuûwen nŋ
haɨ kpasi kɑ̄ nmang-nmang ha owire nŋ̄ dhongwaa kɑ̄û Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɑn, owire nŋ gan ɑ nŋ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha yɑra mbŋti kɑ̄
«Dŋ̄yaâmɑ̄îdŋy kɑ̄ oBetom» bhɑɑ. Nŋ t̄ kɑ ombŋti riito nŋ Luke yɑrâi, a
tɑɨwâ wen ha nma wire nŋ gan ɑ nŋ Zuwif nâ go yinaa ha Teyofili.
Luke gan zɑk Zezi nŋ rip ŋʉ wantŋ nâ, gʼaɨ kidho wen zu d̄yâzan kɑ̄
Zezi n̄ dhongwaa âkâ owire nŋ zɑkâ Zezi nŋ ripʼrôi sokʼaɨ yɑra nŋ
mbŋtii.

Luke uso Zezi tŋ zan ôrô Bem kɑ̄ wire nŋ tŋyâ wen kidhi owire
nŋ yɑwâ dhongwaa kʼôrô sidhayi ga wen zam kpasi kɑʼ̄ wa (19:10).
Nʼaɨ usô Zezi tŋ zan ôrô wire nŋ zɑk ala kɑ̄ owire nŋ owire zɑk wa
ôrô gbɑlɑ wire gai: Owikɑlŋ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nŋ Fŋ̄ngâ-
Sɑɨĵdan dŋyâ zang kɑ̄ omɑ nŋ Zezi dŋyâi bhɑ. Dedhe Nuûwen nŋ Luke
yɑrâ nŋ mbŋtii duro gan ombŋti sɑn nŋ wa yɑra kɑ Mbe-Ndɑkŋi.

Luke kuwɑ nângâ yɑɑ mbŋti kʼɨi nŋ dhongwaa buûrô dhong owen
ngŋÂlŋ̄ng-ngŋ̄lŋ̄ng zu koy kɑ̄ Zezi, kɑraa nʼaq tɑʉwâ wen zu onma mɑ
nŋ dŋyâ mɑ tŋ̄ wese nŋ Zezi kuwɑ nângaâ tom kʼŋɨ zû nu nɑ̄ɑ̄i. A tɑʉwâ
wen dɑ̄ka zu omɑ nŋ Zezi dŋya zû nu nɑ̄ɑi, nʼaq tɑʉwâ wen bhɑ zu fey
kʼaɨ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɑ̄wa kʼaɨ kŋngbarâ tŋ ofey.

Tŋ sɑnkoy mbŋtii, Luke tɑʉwâ wen zu dhongwaa nŋ Zezi dungɑ
kpa nŋ̄ nmaa in owiwemɑ kʼŋʉɨ wâaâ dɑ̄ka-dɑ̄ûka kɑraa nʼaɨ tunɑ ha tÂ
feyi. Luke tɑʉwâ wen bhɑ zu dhongwaa nŋ Zezi dhafa owiwemɑ kʼɨ
wen usi Dedhe Nuûwen ha owirei. Kɑraa nʼaɨ tɑʉwâ wen zu dhongwaa
nŋ Nzapa baa n̄ Zezi tŋ ripzan mŋi.
