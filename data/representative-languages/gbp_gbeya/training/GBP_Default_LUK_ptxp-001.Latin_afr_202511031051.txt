[LUKE

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ̂ Luke

Wire nɛ yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A yɔra mbɛti nɔ̂ɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ̂ nmang̰-nman ha owire nɛ̂ dhon̰waa kɔ̂ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha yɔra mbɛti kɔ̂
«Dɛ̂yâmɔ̂dɛy kɔ̂ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke yɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̧wâ wen ha nma wiire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go yinaa ha Teyofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼạ kidho wen zu dɛ̂yâzan kɔ
Zezi nɛ̂ dhon̰waa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼa̧ yɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ̂ wire nɛ tɛyâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ yɔwâ dhon̰waa kʼôrô sidhayi ga wen zam kpasi kɔ̂ wa (19:10)).
Nʼa̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ̂ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô g̰bɔɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ng̰â-
Ş̧̂dan dɛyâ zan kɔ̂ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛyâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
yɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa yɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wɔ nân̰â yɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhonwaa bûrô dhon owen
nĝlɛ̂n̰-nĝlɛ̂ng zu koy kɔ̂ Zezi, kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛyâ mɔɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi ku̧wɔ nânâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ̂i. A tɔ̧wâ
wen dɔ̂ka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛya zû nu nɔ̂̂i, nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu fey
kʼạ zu san̰karâng te in tûnɔ̂wa kʼa̧ kɛng̰barâ tɛ ofey.

Tɛ sɔnkoy mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhonḛwaa nɛ Zezi dun̰ɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔɔ kʼɛ̰ wââ dɔ̂ka-dɔ̂ka kɔraa nʼa̧ tunɔ ha tɛ̰
feyi. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhonḛwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔɔ Kʼɛ̧
wen usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧wâ wen zu dhonḛwaa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
