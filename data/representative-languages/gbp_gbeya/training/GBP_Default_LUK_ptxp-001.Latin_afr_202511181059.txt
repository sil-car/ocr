Dedhe ŊNûwen nɛ

LUKE
̧ɔr nɛ mbɛti

Gɛk wen zu mbɛti kɔ Luke

Wire nɛ ɔra mbɛti nɔɔ ɔ nɛ Luke. A ɔ nɛ ga̧sa wizam zɛ̂rɛ̂, nɛ
ganaa ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ. A ɔra mbɛti nɔɔ wen usi Dedhe Nûwen nɛ
ha̧ kpasi kɔ nmang-nman ha owire nɛ̂ dhon̰waa kɔ Zezi-Kristi,
gan sɔn, Owire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ oZuwif nâi. Luke ha ɔra mbɛti kɔ
«Dɛ̂âmɔdɛ kɔ oBetom» bhɔ. Nɛ tɛ̂ kɔ̧ ombɛti riito nɛ Luke ɔrâi, a̧
tɔ̣wâ wen ha nma wire nɛ gan ɔ nɛ Zuwif nâ go inaa ha Tȩofili.
Luke gan zɔk Zezi nɛ rip ɛ̧ wantɛ nâ, gʼạ kidho wen zu dɛ̂âzan kɔ
Zezi nɛ̂ dhonwaa âkâ owire nɛ zɔkâ Zezi nɛ ripʼrôi sokʼa̧ ɔra nɛ
mbɛtii.

Luke uso Zezi tɛ zan ôrô Bem kɔ wire nɛ tɛâ wen kidhi owire
nɛ ɔwâ dhon̰waa kʼôrô sidhai ga wen zam kpasi kɔ wa (1 9:10).
Nʼa̧ usô Zezi tɛ zan ôrô wire nɛ zɔk ala kɔ owire nɛ owire zɔk wa
ôrô gbɔlɔ wire gai: Owikɔlɛ, okôo in obem. Luke usô tom nɛ Fɛ̂ngâ-
Sɔdan dɛâ zang kɔ omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛâi bhɔ. Dedhe Nûwen nɛ Luke
̧ɔrâ nɛ mbɛtii duro gan ombɛti sɔn nɛ wa ɔra kɔ̧ Mbe-Ndɔkɛi.

Luke ku̧wwɔ nângâ ɔɔ mbɛti kʼɛ̧i nɛ dhongwaa bûrô dhon owen
ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng-ngɛ̂lɛ̂ng zu ko kɔ Zezi, kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̧̣wâ wen zu onma mɔ
nɛ dɛâ mɔ tɛ̂ wese nɛ Zezi kuwɔ nângâ tom kʼɛ̧ zû nu nɔ᷇ɔ̂i. A tɔwâ
wen dɔka zu omɔ nɛ Zezi dɛa zû nu nɔ̂ɔ̂i, nʼa̧ tɔ̣wâ wen bhɔ zu fe
kʼạ zu sangkarâng te in tûnɔwa kʼa kɛngbarâ tɛ ofe.

Tɛ sɔnko mbɛtii, Luke tɔ̣wâ wen zu dhong̰waa nɛ Zezi dungɔ
kpa nɛ̂ nmaa in owiwemɔ kʼɛ̣ wââ dɔka-dɔka kɔraa nʼa tunɔ ha tɛ̂
fei. Luke tɔ̧wâ wen bhɔ zu dhongwaa nɛ Zezi dhafa owiwemɔ kʼɛ̣
wen usi Dedhe Nûwen ha owirei. Kɔraa nʼa̧ tɔ̣wâ wen zu dhong̰waa
nɛ Nzapa baa nɛ̂ Zezi tɛ ripzan mɛi.
