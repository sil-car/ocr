Nma wese nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ̧ gan sɔn lam na.
Ɔ rɔk nma wire dung ka̧ tɛ wizɛrɛi.

Kɔɔ nɔwa yina nɛ wizam-zɛrɛ ha̧a̧'a̧,
nmaa nɛ zɛrɛi gan kɔ ku̧kutiya, g'a̧ ɔ̧y,
nɛ wey tɛ a̧ gan ga̧ na.
Zey yege, bo ɔ gɔ̧y wende gan sɔ̧danaa hɔwa ha tɛ a̧,
go tɛ a̧ na̧k oro wire nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ fiyo-biri ga,
nɛ ɔ rɔ̧k wi si nɛ a̧ dhong lam tɛ tuwa-zam-zɛrɛ.
