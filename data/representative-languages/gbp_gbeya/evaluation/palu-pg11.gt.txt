Ɔ rɔ̧k ɛrɛ zey nɛ'rɛ dɛ omɔ nɛ wizam-zɛrɛ
tɔ̧ ha'rɛ nɛ dhongwara, zey yege,
bo'rɛ kɔ-dhonga ha zɛrɛ k'ɛrɛ sɔn lam.

Wi ngɛm tɛ'i wenkɔ dhongwaa nɔwa
yina zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ̧ ɔ zang-zang:
kɔ osɔka wire ɔ ɛɛnɛ,
nɛ kɔ obem ɔ sɛnɛ bhɔ.
Nɛ onuwey zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ̧ yoo dɔk.
Zɛy yege, zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ̧i bo ɔ ngay go wizɛrɛi ɔ̧y,
nɛ a kin mɛ ha̧ yina h'a̧ nɛ tan mɛ na.
Mɛ nɛ tɛ wizam-zɛrɛ, nɛ n'a̧ tɔ̧ mbɔra ha mɛ!
