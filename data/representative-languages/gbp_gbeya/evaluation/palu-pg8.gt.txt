Zɔ̧fa ngɛm tɛ ɛ̧ nɛ tɔ̧k tɛ wire.

Go wese nɛ bo n'a̧ kpa tɔ̧k na,
nɛ ganaa rɛm a kuri na.

Nɛ dhongwaa tɔ̧k tɛ owire
a ozɔ̧fa a nɛ kuri k'oro.

Go wese nɛ bo n'a̧ yɔng nma wire
nɛ ɔ nɛ zɛrɛ gɛ̧ɛ̧, n'a̧ kpa obedɔk-zɛrɛi.
Zey yege, bo n'a̧ si dhong, g'a̧ yɔng nma gere wire,
nɛ obedɔk-zɛrɛi nɛ ya zangaai a tɛ tɔ̧k
kɔ mbe wirei, nɛ a̧ bhɔ a̧ kpa zɛrɛi.
