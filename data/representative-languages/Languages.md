## Representative Languages

### Languages List

The languages in this list together cover the most common characters and diacritics that are targeted by Latin_afr. Publications from these languages are used both for training data when training the model, and evaluation data when comparing the model's effectiveness. The less common characters and diacritics are handled by synthetic data, which will not be used for evaluation.

### Representative Documents

- Rasterized documents should be found from each language that contain at least 50 text lines. This is the desired amount for evaluation.
- Any additional lines can be used for training (if text is recognized first),
- Or text from generated documents could be used for synthetic image generation during training.
