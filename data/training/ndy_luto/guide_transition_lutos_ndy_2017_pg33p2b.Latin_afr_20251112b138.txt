nɔn niye desmalɛ, de zaa tarə manɛ. Niye desmalɛ
ma bhoro yoge tɛ: ““ Dɔhi usi gidi ma tuʼ”ʼ, na yoge egi
zagu kovu nzili tɛ: ““ Kori kal rhubha, de zaa, zi ze
gara Zɛrə, Ze Zʼ as kusu gidi de zaa gunu”ʼ. Yogḛ uni
dhovo deri nɛ. Kazə nɔn, Kokudho wo vɔyɔ ngono
